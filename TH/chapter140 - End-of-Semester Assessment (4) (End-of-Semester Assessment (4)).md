“I’m going to leave the war.”

That was how the Guardian of the Empire’s Capital, Obel Forsyth, left the battlefield.

A very brief moment of concern.

A duel was a series of judgments every single second.

Beginner เวทแห่งดวงดาว, Forced Collection.

A field of magic that specialized in reaching ‘results.’ If you could use its power, you could defeat an enemy—no matter how powerful. That was เวทแห่งดวงดาว.

Professor and student.

If you were a magician yourself.
