It was a memory probably more than a year old.

The words she heard then often lingered in ลอร์เทล’s mind.

“Didn’t I tell you before that power gained through betrayal is eventually lost through betrayal? Even though I was so vigilant and careful, it didn’t make any difference.”

“Look at me. Do I look ridiculous?”

“This is your future.”

As she watched the carriage moving away, ลอร์เทล felt a strangely unpleasant feeling.

Those who prospered by betraying others would eventually fall to ruin by being betrayed.

That was a phrase she often heard, from the churches all the way to the merchants. It was a saying that was quite common.

Betrayal was like a magic sword. Once wielded, it stuck to you until death, leaving behind a curse.

Once branded as a traitor, no one would believe in you any longer.

So, it was only when necessary that people continued their relationship with you, based on their own interests.

However, no one wanted to do long-term business with someone who had a history of betrayal, nor would they want to build a relationship of trust that went beyond their personal interests.

If one seized power through betrayal, they must live in solitude until the day someone usurped that power.

The moment one became complacent and lowered their guard, the sword of betrayal that they once wielded would move to another’s hand as they pierced through the original betrayer’s chest.

Once that forbidden weapon fell into your hand, there was no way to escape.

Especially in the world of commerce, where trust and credibility were priceless.

While heading towards your inevitable downfall, struggling to hold on for even one more day… When the time came, you would have no choice but to face your death humbly.

Until that day came, no one would believe in ลอร์เทล เคเฮล์น.

At least in the world of merchants, those who ascended to power through betrayal always ended up like that.

The pouring rain.

For her, who always smiled like a snake, it was a pretty serious expression.

“Yeah, but what’s so different about me? Of course, I’m also afraid. My skills in magic, my talent at conversation, and my past achievements will all be in vain.”

ลอร์เทล had lived in the cruel world of merchants since she was a child, when she could barely read.

“If I lose money and power, what will I have left? Both me and those around me, it will all be empty.”

“You don’t have to think that way.”

“Thanks for trying to make me feel better, but I also know how to view myself objectively to some extent.”

Every time she went to โอลเด็ค, she felt that reality ate at her more and more.

After all, ลอร์เทล’s authority came from money and power.

Her numerous achievements at a young age and the skill at bringing profits into the hands of people, that was why people looked up to her and lowered their heads.

The employees of the store, her business partners, the heads of her competition, the head of โอลเด็ค’s back alley, and even the captain in charge of the ship… The core of every human relationship ลอร์เทล had built was through money and power.

In เบลล์’s eyes… ลอร์เทล looked extremely lonely.

She was at an age where she shouldn’t know about the harsh realities of this world yet. In a romantic place such as ซิลเวเนีย, she was at an age where she should have dreams and goals to reach far away.

She ended up becoming an adult much too early, as she had to stand up on the road by herself with nothing.

It couldn’t have been an enjoyable experience.

“So, I am risking my life to protect my vested interests. I don’t have any great reasons, such as accumulating wealth or proving my ability.”

“Because for me, this is all I have.”

For a bard who traveled the world lightly, a lute around their neck was the best treasure.

For beggars who walked the streets, an empty can used to collect coins was the best treasure.

ลอร์เทล was no different. However, hers was only slightly larger.

To her, that was all she had left. So, holding onto it tightly, afraid that someone might snatch it away… Were they any different than a child’s tantrums?

Was it too much of a stretch to compare her crude inner self, which was like a sly and heartless fox, to that of a child’s tantrums?

With a forced smile, ลอร์เทล hovered by the fireplace.

“But don’t you also have นายน้อย เอ็ด?”

“……”

“At the very least, I think นายน้อย เอ็ด looks at you as yourself.”

เบลล์ was sharp. She always hit where you least expected it.

“That’s why you’re obsessed with เอ็ด, aren’t you?”

A boy who was always sitting by the campfire, sharpening his bow with a dagger.

He was a man who somehow seemed to be out of touch with reality.

Whether it was three coins or piles of gold, he always looked back with blank eyes. It seemed as if he was always just greeting ลอร์เทล truthfully.

He was a person who was completely out of touch with ลอร์เทล’s views of the world.

From the cold streets of โอลเด็ค to เกาะอาเคน, where ซิลเวเนีย was located.

Trust that can be exchanged without cost. How much would you have to pay for something like that?

How valuable was having mutual trust that could be obtained without money?

“There are quite a few people like นายน้อย เอ็ด in the world.”

If there was a slight change, the relationship between them would end up being defined by profit and loss… It would become a transactional relationship.

So, if such a relationship, one based on transaction, formed… It would be no different than a relationship that was maintained through money.

Maintaining that balance was a suffocating and stressful job for ลอร์เทล, who had learned everything while living in the world of merchants.

Whatever way things turned, she was afraid that even the slightest twist would destroy the mutual trust she had built with เอ็ด.

Fear.

Right. The name of that emotion was fear.

It was like clenching a handful of sand with your fist. Even a little carelessness would make it fall through your fingers, disappearing into dust.

But if she opened her fist too late, there would be nothing left. She’d be lying if she said she wasn’t afraid.

“Actually, recently I’ve been feeling countless different emotions that I haven’t felt before in my entire life.”

“I see. You have been in a lot of pain.”

“No, it’s not painful. Rather, it might be better to say that I am happy.”

Feelings of loss, jealousy, monopoly, love, fear, ecstasy, heart pounding, tension, anxiety, a sense of accomplishment, and relief.

It felt like pouring lubricant into a heart that had hardened like a cold machine after living in the cruel world of merchants.

She had spent her entire life as a merchant who built walls, drew lines, and observed others’ behaviors from the outside—regardless of who it was.

For ลอร์เทล, those new thoughts and feelings were rather special. It was as if she was a blind person who had finally come to see the world in full color.

If every moment that ลอร์เทล spent pushing and pulling with เอ็ด was like a treasure for her, then it also had its own value.
