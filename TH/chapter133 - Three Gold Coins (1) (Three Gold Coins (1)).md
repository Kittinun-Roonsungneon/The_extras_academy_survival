“เอ็ด looks a bit depressed these days.”

Obel Hall was a facility used entirely by the สภานักเรียน.

It was in an ideal location, right next to the student square. The exterior of the building was clean and stylish, and there were also several facilities inside that members of the student council could utilize. It was rather large and in good condition for how little people were using it.

As the สภานักเรียน was thoroughly supported by various distinguished individuals as well as noble children, the school also needed to put a lot of consideration into it. That was why it received such a large amount of funds.

The members of the student council were good at networking, achieved impressive grades, and successfully completed their tasks up to the end of their office term, so the student council had earned a lot of trust.

And after graduation, it was common for many of the members to move into important positions within the empire. That was why there was already much support coming from a variety of influential individuals.

So, with the student council’s budget within the school, the various private donations they received, and the student council fee paid for by noble children, the amount of funding they had was impressive. It was at a level where one would wonder if it was really possible for students to manage a budget of such a size.

Of course, a single advisor was appointed to provide minimal assistance as needed, but their influence wasn’t at a level that they could directly affect the decisions made by the student council.

“My older brother…? Looks depressed?”

“The other day I went to his camp, and he said he was fine, but… It felt as if he was uneasy. This is only my gut feeling, though.”

ซิกส์ ไอเฟลสไตน์, a second year in the magic department, was already considered an ace among the student council members.

Rather than feeling a great sense of belonging for the student council, to him it felt as if he was just doing some extra work on his way.

That was because ซิกส์ had a strong sense of freedom. He wasn’t the type of person to belong to one place.

In fact, ซิกส์ treated his work as if he were taking care of ทาเนีย’s personal requests rather than being ordered by the student council president. He didn’t even show up to Obel Hall that often.

ทาเนีย didn’t ask him to change his attitude though. Just the fact that ซิกส์ was a part of the student council was already a great asset.

“He’s depressed? In what way, specifically…?”

The current student council president of สถาบันซิลเวเนีย, ทาเนีย โรสเทย์เลอร์, put down her quill.

The desk in the student council presidents’ personal office was rather large. However, so many documents piled up that it didn’t even matter.

There were a pile of things that needed to be taken care of every day. Every day, when she entered Obel Hall and carefully looked over the reported documents for the day, her morning flew by.

She knew the position would be busy, but ทาเนีย had no idea it would be to such an extent.

However, since it was her personality that let her get that far, ทาเนีย spent her time every day trying to do everything as thoroughly as possible.

“Hmm… On the outside, he looks the same as usual. He’s still as busy as ever. Living in the camp, he has chores to take care of. He’s also a scholarship student, and studies regularly while attending all his classes… But… Hmm… It still felt as if เอ็ด was carrying a burden in his heart.”

“If you’re the one telling me that, ซิกส์, then there must be a reason. It bothers me that we aren’t sure of it, but… Hmm… It’s just intuition…”

ทาเนีย stretched, letting out a groan as she buried her small frame further into the soft chair.

From ทาเนีย’s point of view, the image of เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์ that came to her mind was quite different from what ซิกส์ had said.

He was someone that felt as if he would somehow be able to solve any problem that came up without anyone realizing it.

It was hard to imagine him feeling sad or depressed, so ทาเนีย was rather curious.

“It’s been a while since I greeted my older brother, so I should go in person and say hello…”

“You have to go to the cathedral’s restoration in the afternoon. You also need to head to หอประชุมทริกซ์ to report on the work the student council has been doing. After dinner, you need to go and receive a report on the status of the student clubs’ meeting held at หอพักลอเรล. Then, at night you have to do your personal studies.”

“…Why do you know all of that?”

“Because I have to listen to the chief secretary lament every day…”

The reason ซิกส์ had to visit เอ็ด in place of ทาเนีย was because she couldn’t find any time to do so personally. That was how busy she was.

“I would be less frustrated if I could just know the reason he’s depressed. Can’t you find out the reason, ซิกส์? This time there’s some extra budget for dispatching our members to investigate things, and if you need to miss class to chase after him, I can also request an excused absence for you.”

“ประธานสภานักเรียน. If I start receiving support from the student council like this rather than moving on my own, it may look like this student council is spending money to personally investigate individual students.”

“Why are there so many restrictions with this stupid presidential position…?”

“That’s just the weight that comes with power.”

ทาเนีย let out a deep sigh as she looked out the large window that filled the entire wall on one side of the office.

In the student council president’s office on the fourth floor of Obel Hall, she could see the entirety of the student square from a glance out the window. As lunch time approached, the students hurriedly ran out like a swarm of ants.

And she represented each and every one of them. Her position wasn’t one she could take lightly.

In fact, no matter what she did in the position, she was always going to feel a burden.

The problem was that เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์ wasn’t the type of person that would end up being depressed or shaken up from a burden, unlike her.

Even if he had the weight of someone’s life on his back…

“My brother… I’m worried about him.”

“I’m also concerned. But actually, I’m worried about something else…”

“What?”

ซิกส์ sat down on the sofa in the corner of the office.

“Actually, เอ็ด isn’t the type of person to completely collapse when he feels a burden or becomes depressed. Time will take care of things. But the problem is the relationships around เอ็ด…”

“His relationships…?”

“This is… In a way, it’s an opportunity… President, aren’t you also well aware of the situation around เอ็ด?”

As she fell deeper into her chair, ทาเนีย’s expression hardened.

Slowly, ทาเนีย began to realize what he was saying. Being surrounded by women might be envious for some people, but to him it was a headache.

Considering เอ็ด’s situation, and how he was being hit on by various talented women, if his seemingly perfect image started to tremble… A large opening would appear.

Since เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์ was the type of person that you could always depend on and lean on, imagine becoming the one that he would rely on instead.

That strange feeling of satisfaction that would rise in your chest… In any case, it was an unavoidable temptation for those that saw him as a man.

He normally never showed a weak side of himself, so just how valuable was this opportunity?

“Well, there are always going to be times when people get a bit depressed. There are some people who get better if you comfort them, but I think for เอ็ด, it would be better if he was left alone.”

“This is a difficult situation…”

“Well, if I stick my head in any further, I will end up crossing the line… So, I’m going to leave it as is.”

ซิกส์ coolly stood up as he patted off his clothes.

“Actually, I didn’t come here to report on that. That is just something I wanted to tell you in passing on my own time.”

“What…?”

“Recently, I was rather suspicious about something, so I went around to look into it. I think I ended up discovering something. Isn’t summer break soon? Once the end-of-semester exams finish next week, then the break will start right away?”

“Yes, that’s right.”

ซิกส์ took out a book from his pocket and threw it on the desk in front of him.

It was hard to see from ทาเนีย’s position, but as she narrowed her eyes and looked closely at it, she could read <Introduction to Elemental Studies>.

The most basic elementary studies book that was used by the second and third years. It was rather thick, filled with important key concepts. It was a steady seller within the school.

“Over the past few years, they’ve been growing quite an influence over เกาะอาเคน. However, it looks like they’re finally starting to show their fangs. And this is probably just the start of it.”

“Bearing their fangs…? Perhaps…”

ซิกส์ nodded.

An organized collection of beginner elemental studies books that was centered around the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้.

In particular, the Introduction to Elemental Studies book was least in demand once exams finished and vacation started. As third year students no longer needed the book, they would sell it. And the price of both the used and new books temporarily hit their lowest rates.

And that was what was currently being hidden away and piled up in the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้’s warehouse… The intention behind it would be clear to anyone.

Buy when it’s cheap and sell when it’s expensive.The basics of all investments. But the story started to change if a major company did it.

Cornering the market was a traditional method, but the results were guaranteed. It wasn’t that they couldn’t have done it up till now, but they just hadn’t. All in order to increase their size and influence within the school’s commercial district first.

“Well, considering the size of their company, they wouldn’t be making a massive profit off of this. But… this is a sort of prelude to things to come.”

“That’s true. Right now, it might just be an Elemental Studies textbook. However, in the future they may expand their scope to clothing, magically engineered items, school supplies, and even more…”

The acting deputy chairman of the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้, ลอร์เทล เคเฮล์น.

ทาเนีย had seen her face a few times already. However, the most meaningful memory she had of her was down the hallway of หอพักโอฟีเลียส, where no one else was.

She still couldn’t breathe properly when she remembered how ลอร์เทล stood there, glaring at her with เอ็ด’s death weighing on her back, waiting for ทาเนีย like a cold corpse.

Not only that, but she was also flawless when it came to ability. Naturally, she was talented with money, but she also had excellent skills in magic and her ability to improvise in a crisis that had already been widely recognized.

However, now that ทาเนีย was the student council president, she was in a position where she needed to keep her in check.
