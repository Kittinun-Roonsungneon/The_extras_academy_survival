A famous aristocrat that used to work in the imperial kitchen, who also owned several famous restaurants in the empire’s capital. Sweating, he gently placed another cup of tea on the table.

Without ordering and receiving supplies from the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้, Laplace Bakery would struggle to operate the following day.

The rest of what she would say was obvious.

Laplace Bakery, who wanted to quickly receive their supplies, raised the amount of product needed by making a fake order. And once the delivery date approached, they canceled the order, only taking what they needed.

It was a shallow decision to think that the number of extra materials ordered wouldn’t be a major issue for the company, as they were so large.

Demand for produce was also high right now. So, all of those canceled orders could be sold elsewhere with no financial loss to the company.

However, right now nearly all of their clients were still waiting for their orders. So, it was a cheap trick to use just so they could get all the supplies they needed first.

ลอร์เทล was someone that rose through the ranks of the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้. Similar to a king of the underworld, at least that was the case here in the commercial district.

And ลอร์เทล’s means of control came from money. Looking at the underbelly of the peaceful commercial district, you could see that in the end all the money flowed into her wallet.
