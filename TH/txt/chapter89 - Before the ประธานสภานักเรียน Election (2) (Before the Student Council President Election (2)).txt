Careful, in case even the tip of his finger got cut, เครปิน โรสเทย์เลอร์ put the dagger into its sheath. He then held it out to his trusted aid.

The aid that politely took the dagger hid it under his robe. Then, the assistants standing on both sides of him knelt in front of เครปิน.

He had told ทาเนีย โรสเทย์เลอร์ in advance through a letter.

It seemed that having her take responsibility for both becoming the student council president and the negotiation for the Sage’s Seal with ลอร์เทล would be too much. So, he decided to send a trusted vassal to assist her. Even though they were being sent out for business reasons, he was sure that the school would allow it.

Though, the real purpose behind sending the vassal wasn’t to help ทาเนีย. Rather, there was a shadier reason hiding beneath the water.

เครปิน returned to his room as he took off the glove on his right hand. He placed his hand, engraved with a hideous pattern, on the crystal ball as a huge amount of magic power erupted from his hand.

One of the powers granted to เครปิน โรสเทย์เลอร์ by the evil god ‘เมบูลา’. Cause and Effect Determination.

It was one of the highest tiers of magic, one that worked by specifying a certain action or variable that you have caused. Then, the results of such action or variable would be projected onto a crystal ball.

It requires a lot of magic power, so it couldn’t be used often. Still, being able to check in advance the consequences of your actions before an important event… It was an incredible ability that was similar to fraud.

Then, before long… the ‘result’ was shown through the crystal ball.

The scene shown was…

A cliff with rain pouring down. It seemed to be a coastal area near the northern part of เกาะอาเคน.

Inside the crystal ball was เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์. Enduring a brutal and bloody battle, until his body was dripping with blood and covered in injuries. Despite enduring for so long, in the end he was stabbed in the abdomen by the dagger that the vassal pulled out.

Dark red blood spurted from his mouth as he took a step back, little by little.

A girl with light pink hair that arrived too late screamed out. The vassal then pulled out the dagger as he pushes เอ็ด away with his foot. And just like that, เอ็ด fell down the distant cliff.

The rain continued to fall on the girl, who ran towards the cliff.

With her head lowered, she slowly turned around, looking back up at the vassal.

And with that, the crystal ball’s projection ended.

“……”

Sitting down at his work table, เครปิน put on his glove again as he let out a sigh.

“This isn’t a great feeling.”

However, the expression on his face didn’t budge.

“But still, this is a necessary sacrifice.”
