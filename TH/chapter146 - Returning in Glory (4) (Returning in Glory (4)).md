The third princess, เพเนีย, returned to the โครเอล Imperial Family.

It had been more than half a year since เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย, who had always stayed within the Imperial Household, had left for สถาบันซิลเวเนีย.

The way she always walked around the Imperial Household beautifully, caring for the servants with the utmost kindness, was admired by everyone.

The Imperial Family was worried about เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย spending four years in a foreign land to graduate from school. However, every vacation she would return to the household to ease their worries.

The same was true for her third break since entering ซิลเวเนีย.

All of the guards, mercenaries, and servants paid their respects towards เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย, who they had not seen in a while. เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย was the same as always, with her gorgeous platinum blonde hair, a beautiful dress that suited her, and her dignified yet elegant appearance that people admired.

“That’s true. My body is not only mine. However, you don’t look all that healthy either.”

เพเนีย was suddenly speechless.

เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย returned to the imperial family in a fancy outfit with splendid accessories that embodied dignity and nobility. No matter who saw her, she looked just as beautiful and benevolent as she did before she left the household.

However, to จักรพรรดิโครเอล, that didn’t seem to be the case. He looked at เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย with even more focus.

He has been watching over เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย since she was a child. That was why he noticed that the current เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย in front of him was unlike her usual self.

For example, whenever she visited จักรพรรดิโครเอล, she would talk about her experiences and the new things she learned rather than worrying about his health and safety. She was a girl that would become overly excited, telling her stories until she belatedly remembered to ask about the emperor’s health.

เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย’s childhood wasn’t great.

Even though she lived at the peak of power, she was on the receiving end of several secret attacks against her family and authority. However, she continued to show a lively and active appearance in front of จักรพรรดิโครเอล. Such strength was one of the reasons จักรพรรดิโครเอล valued เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย the most.

However, the more time she spent on เกาะอาเคน, the more เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย seemed to gradually be losing that side of her.

It was her third vacation since entering ซิลเวเนีย. Now, จักรพรรดิโครเอล was beginning to worry about her.

Seeing เพเนีย change little by little was something that was all too familiar to him. After all, he was someone that had seen all sorts of people.

“You seem to be frustrated with your failures.”

Hearing those words, which were right on the money, เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย kept her mouth closed.

She had gained much experience since entering ซิลเวเนีย, and she had even gotten involved in various incidents… However, no matter what she did, things never turned out the way she wanted them to. It was quite different than in the imperial household, where all of her actions worked out as she had planned.

From จักรพรรดิโครเอล’s point of view, it was impossible to know exactly what she went through. It could simply be issues with her grades, or it could be that she was deeply frustrated with human relationships or politics and reality.

Human growth takes place amidst many setbacks. จักรพรรดิโครเอล wasn’t unaware of that fact, but it was still heartbreaking to see his daughter, who was born of the same blood as him, continue to walk down such a path of suffering.

The fact that Crown Prince Lindon had been thrown into disarray because of the fierce battle for the royal court, thus giving up on taking the throne, remained a great wound to จักรพรรดิโครเอล. He didn’t want that history to repeat itself.

“It seems… That I am lacking in too many areas to succeed the throne.”

“เพเนีย.”

“Surely, after being away from the Imperial Household for so long, my position must have also been greatly weakened as well.”

The hierarchy of power within the imperial family was gradually shifting towards the first princess, เซลาห์.

The imperial prime minister, the commander of the general guard, the commander of the knights, the head butler… They were all slowly turning their hearts towards เซลาห์… Only a few key personnel remained under เพเนีย.

In the end, authority was established through subordinates.

It was inevitable that the less people เพเนีย had supporting her, the further away she was from gaining power.

เพเนีย was well aware of that fact. However, she had no intention of re-establishing her foundation.

Chronic lethargy was eating away at เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย.

So, unless they were a person around the level of a member of ตระกูลโรสเทย์เลอร์, she wouldn’t even notice them.

Right now, เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย was the biggest thread to เซลาห์.

If he were a person that เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย had brought over to her side, then เซลาห์ couldn’t easily laugh about the matter.

เซลาห์ crossed her legs as she lifted the teacup with one hand. Her cold gaze turned towards the rim of the cup.

Her bare leg stretched out, tapping the edge of the wooden desk.

“Is he a person of such worth?”

That question lingered in her chest.

“I don’t know.”

There was no criteria when it comes to evaluating another’s judgment of someone. Or rather, one shouldn’t blindly trust another’s judgment.

เซลาห์ was a person who saw and judged whatever happened on her own. However, she could still refer to another’s evaluation.

“Well, I guess it’s something to think about during the social gathering. After all, it will begin tonight.”

“How should I prepare your outfit, เจ้าหญิงเซลาห์?”

“I’ll leave it to your discretion.”

Then, when it became evening, everyone would gather back together again for the social gathering. As one repeated that schedule for five days, there would only be more and more people arriving, making things even more hectic.

เซลาห์ has a history of making the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้’s Kroelon branch review the tax exemption bill time and time again. She was also in a position where she could use the imperial family’s power to strangle him at any moment.

Valvern, the master of Alchemy who resided in the land of alchemy, Krett. She had no connection with him. He was a research fanatic who lived his life buried away.

She needed to build up her relationship with him from the ground up.

“Shall I prepare a present to give him? I’ve been told that alchemists prefer practical gifts rather than ceremonial ones. I’m sure he would enjoy receiving new test tubes or consumable supplies that are nicely packaged.”

“Could you prepare it right now? We are in the คฤหาสน์โรสเทย์เลอร์ right now, after all.”

“Valvern arrives on the fourth day, so I believe I can manage it just on time.”

“Then, please do so.”

After saying that, เซลาห์ put the papers down.

Seh smiled coldly as she gently shook her feet.

“Should I go and talk with the head of House Bloomriver tonight?”

Of course, that was what she needed to do at the social gathering that night. But right now, the sun is still high in the middle of the sky.

“But before that… Hm…”

Boom!

A dozen wolves, which appeared through hallucination magic, were instantly enveloped in flames as they disappeared.

The ice spear that was cast instantly afterwards was blocked by an enormous amount of magic power.

Even though it was magic that was cast sincerely, Sinir Bloomriver—a white-haired middle-aged woman who was the head of House Bloomriver—was completely surprised. However, she didn’t let it show.

There, เครปิน โรสเทย์เลอร์ sat with his children next to him… เอ็ด and ทาเนีย.

Sinir let out words of admiration.

House Bloomriver was a noble family of the empire that was at the peak of magic.

Particularly, they were a house that focused on practical combat magic to emerge victorious from a battle, rather than digging into the academic field and researching magic.

In order to win, they also used cunning and wicked magic. After all, House Bloomriver was known as ‘The Witch’s House’ within the empire.

That was because the head of the house, Sinir Bloomriver, was wicked in her own way, just like a witch.

However, in the face of overwhelming power, all of those tactics were nothing more than miscellaneous techniques.

It was hard to believe, but it seemed as if that girl had skills in magic that even surpassed Sinir, who had been the head of the mighty House Bloomriver for a long time. And she was only a student.

She was the one who started the duel.

That was because the previous winter break, she heard stories about ลูซี่ เมย์ริล, a student at ซิลเวเนีย.

The current fourth year’s top student in the magic department, ทราเซียนา บลูมริเวอร์, and her twin sister, Patriciana Bloomriver.

Both were members of House Bloomriver and were children of Sinir.

The results were beyond her imagination.

Sinir couldn’t even land a scratch on ลูซี่.

She continued to walk away towards เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์, sitting down in his lap. She didn’t pay attention to the glances coming her way.

Seeing her bury her head in his chest, falling asleep, it was as if that overwhelming power she showed just moments ago was all just a dream.

“…You have quite the strange friend.”

“Yes, she’s quite strange.”

Even เครปิน had to admit it after seeing ลูซี่’s performance. She was beyond his expectations.

Sinir Bloomriver was someone who was recognized and held authority beyond the highest rated magician within the Imperial Household. Thus, it was objective evidence that ลูซี่ had already far surpassed the level of a student. She was strong enough to easily enter the ranks of the most powerful within the Empire.

All at that age… she wasn’t even an adult yet. No one knew what kind of monster she would become as she grew older.

A person who could make the great and noble เครปิน wait just because they were tired. Aside from จักรพรรดิโครเอล, could such a person possibly exist in this world?
