It was the period after the student council election had ended, and ทาเนีย โรสเทย์เลอร์’s new cabinet was starting to take shape—to some extent. Chronologically, it was about ten days after the election ended.

“Are you dating เอ็ด?”

No matter who saw it, the Spiritual Studies senior professor Melina had a rather poor appearance.

Her long, plain black hair was neatly combed down, but her tired eyes and pouty mouth gave her a deeply dissatisfied look. Even the way she dressed, which showed off the curves of her body, made her look more like an inn owner than a professor.

She was holding a pipe as she breathed out smoke. She looked very intimidating to get close to.

“…What…?”

“Are you dating เอ็ด?”

She had called เยนิกา to help review the first- and second-year students’ spiritual studies assignments.

That was why เยนิกา had come all the way to her private lab in the education district, but that was the first thing she asked.

With เยนิกา’s personality, she couldn’t help but be flustered.

“A-A-A-A-A-All of a sudden…?? Why are you saying that…?”

“Are you joking? Aside from spiritual studies, the only class you two ever shared was elemental studies, yet you’re always stuck together. Whether you go to the student welfare facility or the student cafeteria to eat something, you’re always stuck together with him.”

“…That’s… Uhm…”

“Even if, as a man and woman, you meet at the same eye level—you’re still a girl that has all these circuits of love and dating running around your head. At this point, aren’t there already rumors going around about you two?”

Professor Melina shivered for a bit as her teeth trembled, before exhaling the smoke once more.

“And these days, whenever you have time, you start fidgeting around with a smile on your face… Even during lectures, you just blankly stare at the blackboard while smiling bashfully. You are just like a little kid that has just started dating. I can’t help but sigh. Just what the hell is with you? What are you thinking about for the corners of your mouth to wiggle around like that? That is what I’m trying to say.”

“Y-You don’t have to say that much…!”

It was definitely a sharp observation. Whether it was her absentminded humming or the airy, clueless tone in her voice, เยนิกา did seem rather excited these days.

It was all because, due to เอ็ด’s seclusion, the amount of time the two of them spent together increased quite a bit.

Basically, since เอ็ด barely had any friends that he was close to, when he was at school he spent most of his time with เยนิกา.

There were a few classes that they took together, they ate together, and they did homework together—and before she realized it, they were always sticking together.

At the very least, it was fortunate that happiness itself wasn’t lethal.

“Alright… As a professor, no matter who you meet and what you do I shouldn’t be meddling in your business. I just called you today to ask for help with grading the first- and second- year students’ spiritual resonance assignments.”

“Grading…? Me…?”

“There’s no issue, right? I’ll even give you extra points.”

As far as elementalism was concerned, it was safe to say there were a very miniscule number of people in the school that could keep up with เยนิกา.

Of course, an old eagle was still better than a young crow. Since Melina was a professor, her knowledge of elementalism—her field of specialization—was unmatched.

However, it was difficult to say that the scope of her talents went beyond the realm of academics.

The spirit of elementalism. History. The ecosystem of spirits. Concepts of spiritual affinity. Resonance training methods. A deep understanding of the concept of resonance.

In terms of academics, naturally เยนิกา was far behind a faculty member that had been studying her entire life… The realm of their knowledge was completely different.

But just as knowing a sport well and being good at it were two entirely different things… When looking at practical skills, เยนิกา’s innate senses that let her freely handle even high-ranking spirits was enough to make even the stingy Melina bow her head.

“The joint combat practice is scheduled to take place soon, so consider this an opportunity to establish your spot within your grade.”

“I don’t want to establish something like that…”

“You really don’t even want to do that? Seriously, even in class you’re off smiling like an idiot, as if you’re the happiest person in the world. Can’t you at least get a bit of a grip? Being able to enjoy your youth with flowers blooming around you is your luck, but from the point of view of someone watching you, they can’t help but think ‘Is she really out of her damn mind?’”

After hearing her talk up to that point, เยนิกา’s face started to heat up for some reason. She quickly nodded.

Once she fell into her own world, she had a tendency to be completely unaware of her surroundings. Maybe that was why people around her had started to express their concerns whenever they saw her off by herself in a strangely good mood.

In actuality, it wasn’t something for others to be concerned about. Just because the corners of her lips kept moving on their own, it wasn’t like they were going to fall off.

However, there was one fact that เยนิกา did not take into account.

Looking at her from a third point of view, seeing her walk around the school alongside เอ็ด and flirting with him… It would look like they were lovers. There was nothing strange with that thought process at all. One day, if the two of them were to suddenly say ‘we’re dating’, everyone would just naturally accept it.

That fact wasn’t very pleasing to some others.

“……”

อานิส Haylan opened her heavy eyes with a sullen expression as she fixed her cloak… never touched her teacup even once.

เยนิกา was quietly photosynthesizing while sitting on the terrace of a tea house in the middle of the student center. As if she were a stuffed toy, she smiled brightly without moving one bit. She looked the happiest in the world, a dazzling expression running across her face.

Nothing more, nothing less, she was just an idiot. It seemed even the flowers around her were blooming.

“You must have worked quite hard today, เยนิกา. Today’s monster ecology assignment was quite difficult, since there was so much.”

“Hm? Uh yeah~”

“…I should eat dinner early. Before the sun goes down, I’ll have to head back to the dorm to try and decipher the ancient text that Professor Stray gave me. Hearing about it in class, it seemed difficult to take care of by myself. Still, I should at least give it a try…”

“Yeah~ That’s right~ We should at least give it a try~ The detoxification class didn’t give much homework this time though~ So it should be fine~”

“……”

As usual, เยนิกา listened to อานิส, acknowledging what she was saying and responding to her. But this time, อานิส felt strangely angry.

อานิส sat at the table across from her with a smile on her face, but it felt as though the veins in her forehead were starting to bulge.

It wouldn’t be strange for อานิส to smile looking at เยนิกา’s happy face, so why was she slightly angry all of a sudden?

A little reflection of her feelings would have quickly revealed the reason, but อานิส didn’t deal with it right away.

“By the way, เยนิกา… About that guy…”

“Hm? You mean เอ็ด…?”

“Yeah. เยนิกา, you must have known from the beginning that he wasn’t dead, right?”

At first, อานิส was worried that เยนิกา had lost her mind seeing her cook for no apparent reason. But she suddenly understood all her actions.

เยนิกา had kept it a secret, even though she already knew that เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์ wasn’t dead. Since it was only เยนิกา and not anybody else that knew, there must have been a convincing reason.

อานิส didn’t fully understand that fact, so she was a bit upset about it all.

“I… I was really worried about you back then, เยนิกา. I had thought ‘The death of that guy was causing you so much heartache that you must be struggling so much.’ After all, you’re my dear friend, เยนิกา.”

“Y-Yeah…”

“That’s why… I feel a little bit pissed off right now. I was worrying about you so much, yet you knew the truth all along and kept watching me worry about you. Right?”

“N-No… Th-That’s…”

What are you saying right now to เยนิกา, one of your most precious friends in this entire world? Why are you saying such a meaningless thing?

อานิส didn’t even understand what she was doing. She was only saying whatever came out of her mouth.

“That, I-I’m sorry… อานิส. But I really had a reason for it.”

“To the point you couldn’t even tell me…?”

“Y-Yeah…”

“Then… It can’t be helped…”

อานิส sighed deeply. As she kept her head down, she glanced At เยนิกา’s eyes with a glare to the side.

Since เยนิกา always was the kindest person in the world, she was acting restless and guilty, quickly tapping her fingertips.

She felt a sharp pain piercing her chest. A decent amount of ingratitude had the power to incite one’s deviation.

“Since it was you, เยนิกา, that guy เอ็ด must have been caught up in a serious situation.”

“S-Something like that…”

She said it was similar, but it was spot on accurate. Inferring something based on the limited information available was อานิส’s specialty.

“In the first place, เยนิกา… it seemed that you were only helping him every day and giving things to him…”

She felt strange because she was speaking quite directly, but either way it was still a fact.

“เยนิกา, are you his maid…? Even if he was hiding, what were you doing cooking food for him and taking care of his housing…? Did you even go and give him your money or something…? Did he even give you a hug…? Even though you said that he is the first person you gave your heart to, don’t you think you’re crossing the line too much…!”

“Th-That’s…”

“Like I told you before, you can get thrown away in an instant after he no longer has any use for you…! In fact, is เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์ the only man left in this world…??”

“Why are you saying bad things about เอ็ด all of a sudden…? อานิส, I told you last time, but เอ็ด is a good person…!”

“Eughk…!”

อานิส was speechless. In the first place, it felt as if she had just been blurting out whatever came to mind. Because of that, what she was saying was incoherent and logically inconsistent.

อานิส knew all too well just how decent and thoughtful เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์ was compared to she had once thought. That was why it was heartbreaking for her to see the conversation between them flowing in a strange direction, as if she was only talking bad about him.

However, as mentioned before, a decent amount of ingratitude had the power to incite one’s deviation. In particular, those that lived a steady life, only choosing the correct choices, could start to feel strangely attracted to the strange twists on the road once they experienced it once.

“I-I didn’t mean to say those things… S-Sorry…”

“N-No…”

Silence came over for a moment.

The atmosphere turned strangely delicate. Neither of them quite knew why things had ended up like that.

“S-Still, เยนิกา… There’s nothing wrong with being careful.”

“Careful… About what…?”

“It’s a bit complicated to say. To be honest, when you think about it… Even though you are so dedicated to him, that guy เอ็ด has never done anything in return, right? Isn’t that the case…?”

“H-He says thank you every time, though. He even patted me on the shoulder and told me that it would have been awful if I hadn’t been there… we also shook hands…”

“In the end, it’s all just talk…!”

It was starting to sound like the mindset of a wife that was domestically abused. Isn’t this too much, is what she started to think… อานิส’s advice then started to take a strange turn.

“Then… It’s time to test that man, เยนิกา.”

“A test… on เอ็ด? Saying that, it seems like you don’t really trust เอ็ด.”

“Now is the time to be suspicious of him. You deserve it, เยนิกา. You didn’t help เอ็ด just once or twice. Are you going to continue living like this? When you can get something, you need to take it! At this rate you’ll really end up a pushover.”

She didn’t really want to do an old-school push and pull strategy like the fox ลอร์เทล had done. It had been quite a while since she gave up on that.

At the very least, it would be a good idea to stop giving him things for a while and keep a bit of distance, limiting herself to a basic greeting.

“That guy, เอ็ด… Make him a bit mad.”

“What…? Me… Why…?”

“People show their true nature when they are angry to their core, เยนิกา.”

That was why she needed to do something to make him angry, to see how things would end up.

It was hard to imagine เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์ getting angry, but he was still human. So, when he got angry, he would surely show it.

“But… เอ็ด has never been mad at me even once…”

เยนิกา spoke while nervously twisting her braids… She didn’t know why, but for some reason, อานิส was acting rather rude…!

If she were her regular self, there wouldn’t be a reason to act so rude… That was all it was.

“And it’s not polite to go around testing a person by trying to make them mad…”

The logic she was expecting had been brought up. However, there wasn’t a reason to try and argue against it.

“เยนิกา. Listen well. To put it bluntly, you have a crush on that guy, right?”

“Hiccup.”

No matter how aware of your emotions you were, it’s human psychology to feel a bit shy when they were put into words.

Without blinking, อานิส’s words came rushing out revealing the truth. เยนิกา swallowed her saliva as she blushed.

“But now, don’t you realize that you are getting closer to him as a close friend or family member rather than his lover…?”

“Th-That’s…”

It was truly a point.

The warm and comfortable feeling that came to her mind when she thought of เอ็ด’s face, or imagined sitting down next to him in the camp.

Imagining leaning on his shoulder, before when her heart pounded as blood rushed to her face…These days, the feeling of being comfortable, as if she were returning home, was much more prevalent.

“That’s the problem, เยนิกา! That guy must be feeling the same thing…! What you two need to do is a ‘re-establishment’ of your relationship!”

“Re-establishment…?”

“That’s right… You guys need to act a little ‘unfamiliar!’ If you remain the same with each other, you won’t ever get excited!”

อานิส’s words had a strange magic power. At some point, เยนิกา had swallowed her saliva and started to get intrigued by her words.

“That’s why… moderately… you need to make him mad. Then the true version of that guy will show itself.”

Watching เยนิกา swallow hard… อานิส also started to feel a strange burning in her chest.

“W-Well then, I’ll get going…! I’ll see you tomorrow, อานิส…!”

Regardless, เยนิกา had already decided to take a look at เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์’s spiritual resonance training later that day.

Talking about her schedule, as she saw เยนิกา off at the student center, อานิส sat down on a nearby bench with her face covered.

What type of bullshit did she tell her? To go and piss the guy off first? Then she made up some plausible reason like re-establishing their relationship? But in the end, it was all rubbish for เยนิกา to worry about.

That… that was surely human nature.

Her behavior was so embarrassing that อานิส had to sit down on the wooden bench for some time to cool off.

But… on the other hand, another question started to arise in her heart. A question that at the very least neutralized her strangely rising feeling of being an immoral and guilty person.

In the first place… Was it even possible for เยนิกา to make him angry…?

“……”

In conclusion, it was absolutely impossible.

In the first place, เยนิกา wasn’t the type of person who could do something like that. That was just the kind of person she was.

Even more so if the person she was trying to make angry was เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์.

“Seriously… doing something useless…”

อานิส slapped her cheeks, then swept her hand over face.
