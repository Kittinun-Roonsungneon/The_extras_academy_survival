Her body was covered in dust and dirt, but she didn’t shed a single drop of blood. Her oversized shirt and skirt were torn, becoming ragged. And the amount of magic power she had, which was enough to flip over the entire world, had decreased considerably.

And yet, ลูซี่ เมย์ริล had no intention of collapsing.

Kwaaaaaaak!

Casting ultimate elemental magic. Who knows how many times she has cast it already.

How much strength could a single human have? It was amazing that there was such a tremendous power in a human who had lived less than a hundred years.

However, there was no reason for เมบูลา to end its life there.

เมบูลา had lost all reason to descend.

It was supposed to descend upon the world through countless sacrifices. However, the medium that was meant to offer them had died.

Despite เมบูลา giving him an endless amount of its magic power, he eventually met the end of his life at the hands of เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์.

“……?”

The magic circle เมบูลา cast broke away to a place outside the clutches of humans… Passing into the dimension that เมบูลา had originally come from.

เมบูลา had decided to retreat. All because of that one magician.

“Where…!”

ลูซี่ gathered magic powers in her hand once more in front of เมบูลา, who was about to retreat. A magic circle of that level could be destroyed if ลูซี่ concentrated on it.

However, feeling her stiff shoulders suddenly throb in pain… ลูซี่’s frowned.

She looked down at คฤหาสน์โรสเทย์เลอร์ with blank eyes. It looked as if เครปิน โรสเทย์เลอร์ had already died and the chaos inside the mansion had been taken care of.

The servants and tentacles lost all their strength as they collapsed. The gremlins also retreated as the Knight Templar and Imperial Guards entered the mansion.

The situation was coming to an end. In the end, เมบูลา couldn’t defeat ลูซี่ before เครปิน had died.

A single human hand blocked the path of an Evil God… There was no need to explain what a feat that was.

“Fine. Go.”

All of เมบูลา’s eyes looked directly at ลูซี่ as it disappeared into the magic circle. It was as if it was declaring this was simply a temporary retreat. However, the fact that it was defeated in the end didn’t change.

“Don’t even think about coming back.”

Under the bright stars, ลูซี่’s white hair fluttered in the wind. She couldn’t tie it like usual, so seeing her with her hair flying in the wind like that left an impression.

Looking at เมบูลา with a blank expression on her face, it didn’t look like she particularly felt joy over her victory. In the end, it seemed as if she felt incomplete from the fight that ended in a rush.

As เมบูลา disappeared into the night sky, ลูซี่ slowly descended to the ground.

“She’s coming down…!”

“That monster… She dealt with it on her own…!”

“H-How many ultimate magic spells did she use…? It was a number that couldn’t be counted… Yet she’s completely fine…?”

As she slowly descended from the night sky landing in the central garden, the soldiers began to split up.

Seeing that girl come down to earth after fighting against that evil god on her own, she looked like a messenger of God.

She was a person who could easily destroy the entire mansion with just a single wave of her hand if she so desired.

That fact had been clearly proven in her battle. The perpetrators were in a situation where they couldn’t say a word.

Though, ลูซี่ didn’t pay attention to those gazes as she ripped off the ends of her tattered clothes.

She looked around. With her eyes wide open, she could only see soldiers. However, closing her eyes and feeling the flow of magic power, she smelt that familiar grassy scent somewhere in the distance.

Fixing her clothes, she ran through all the soldiers, who were staring at her. Wherever she went, the crowd of people made way for her, clearing the path.

She found a blonde boy lying under a large zelkova tree in the central garden.

His body was covered in blood as he sat there unconscious, leaning against the tree… It looked as if he went through a lot.

There were two soldiers nearby giving first aid as an elementalist watched from behind. There was even the Saint as well. Seeing those two girls, she glared at them. She eventually decided that it was fine as she exhaled through her nose, walking proudly over to the boy.

“Ah…!”

One of the soldiers who was giving first aid looked back, startled. The same went for the elementalist and saint next to them.

He tried to say something to ลูซี่, but as if she weren’t interested, it went straight through to the other ear. She pushed the soldier aside as she put her finger on the boy’s chin.

Cuts, burns, bruises, lacerations, and even fractures, he had already endured many hardships before, but this was the first time he suffered that level of injury.

And the flow of magic power inside of him was completely twisted, as he had gathered too much. His temperature was rapidly rising because of the recoil. It was heartbreaking just to look at him, but all ลูซี่ could do right now was to inject her own magic power into him to unravel his twisted magic power.

After adjusting the flow of magic power for a long time, ลูซี่ leaned back on the zelkova tree as she sat down next to เอ็ด.

Both of them were in bad shape. ลูซี่ was able to move her body around enough, but she was completely exhausted.

ลูซี่ rested her head on เอ็ด’s shoulder as she sighed. And because of the exhaustion that came from using her magic power to its limit… her eyes started to close little by little.

“On the other side of the mansion… my hat… take care of it…”

After saying that, she quietly closed her eyes. And slowly, the sound of her soft breathing could be heard.

The soldiers and two girls were left speechless as they watched her. What they were sure of was that, right now, no one could touch ลูซี่.

She was the hero of the calamity, and on her own she managed to fight and help stop the descent of the Evil God, sending it back.
