หอพักโอฟีเลียส

Staff Room Terrace.

On the contrary, when she became the head maid, the number of hours the maids had to work decreased and the amount of time they had to rest increased. That ultimately contributed to an overall increase in work efficiency.

“Before then… I’ll have to bring back young คุณหนูลูซี่…”

After being released from probation, ลูซี่, who was running around outside of หอพักโอฟีเลียส, needed to be brought back and put to sleep.

That was because in order to prepare for the school’s inspection tomorrow morning, it was necessary to start with ลูซี่, who was the top student and representative of her school year.

Since her work had become somewhat normalized, she thought it would be better to go and see if เอ็ด was doing okay while on her way to pick up ลูซี่.

“Hmm…”

However, whenever she thought of เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์, a complicated relationship came to her mind.

She would like to think that it had all been sorted out to some extent, but considering his actions she wasn’t sure about that.

Taking off her headband for a moment and leaning against the railing on the terrace, she looked up at the starry sky.

Quietly enjoying the cool night breeze of late spring, she suddenly realized that there was no time more pleasant than this to live in the wild.

What kind of person was เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์?

Wasn’t he the type of person that was always living with his back against the corner, unable to afford to pay any attention to human relationships? All because he was busy trying to survive and live his own life?

However, enjoying this cool night’s breeze, she started to have a new thought.

Looking at his camp recently, she began to realize that things had changed quite a bit.

Compared to the past, when he struggled and clenched his teeth while living in the wild with only his wooden shelter and campfire… Now there was a decently built cabin, a simple warehouse made of timber, a fairly large campsite, and various tools and materials that piled up around it. Fishing tools and net farms were set up across the river, well-organized hunting traps were put here and there in the forest, and various magically engineered items that were useful for his life were also prepared.

He had many different types of food ingredients, so it seemed like he was quite good at cooking. And หอพักโอฟีเลียส was helping him here and there with things like clothing.

In short, after more than a year of hard work, เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์… now had a place that was ‘good enough to live in.’ Although the environment was still relatively poor, the environment had been improved to the extent that he would no longer have to live while clenching his teeth. It was the result of his hard work.

Time to relax and rest his body… That would lead to his peace of mind. It meant that he now had more time to think about things other than his survival.

“I’m not sure…”

However, เบลล์ started to have her doubts again. In the first place, wasn’t he a man that was like an iron wall? Thinking about whether or not if someone out there could get through to someone like that, she couldn’t think of anyone. If it were possible, then just being intimate with him wouldn’t be enough.

An anomalous attack from a different direction. She wondered if a relationship forming from an unfamiliar and new point of view would be a more effective means… Though, she couldn’t think of anyone that could carry out such a high-level attack.

It was not something just anyone could do. If someone did it, it would probably have come from an accident rather than an intentional action. It went without saying, but the chances of that were quite slim.

“It’s a pity…”

With the cool breeze of late spring, เบลล์ มายาร์ prepared to head out.

Either way, if that man’s iron wall temperament was lowering just a little, wouldn’t now be the right time to attack? She was hoping that someone would be able to draw the same conclusion. Because right now, it felt like her heart was giving out just watching it all.

“Isn’t joint combat training coming up…? I hope no one gets hurt this year…”

Hoping that the relationships around เอ็ด would all be sorted out… เบลล์ prepared to go pick up ลูซี่.

How long would that meaningless war of nerves last…? No one could say for sure. After coming that far, it unknowingly started to feel like a battle of pride.

“I’m glad that young Lady เยนิกา also seems to be in good health since she transferred to หอพักเด็กซ์.”

เบลล์ was the maid that เยนิกา had the closest relationship with while living in หอพักโอฟีเลียส. Back then she was only a senior maid, but they seem to be happy to see each other after such a long time.

“You know, เอ็ด.”

“I-It’s just… I’ve always wanted to try something like this.”

“……”

What was so embarrassing that she needed to keep her head down like that while sitting up straight and being quiet?

It wasn’t anything major, and it wasn’t like we would be doing it for very long.

I thought that we would only say a few things to each other before I politely said farewell and she went away. หอพักเด็กซ์’s curfew was nearing, so it wouldn’t be much longer before she would have to leave.

เยนิกา took a few deep breaths, as if she was about to do something important, before suddenly put her arms on her knees and sitting modestly. She cleared her throat.

The heat was starting to well up on her face. She seemed to be rather embarrassed about what she was going to do. Suddenly, when she managed to finally say something, her voice was quiet and barely distinguishable. Seeing her like that now, the gap between her usual bubbly and vivid self was too extreme.

I underestimated the destructive power of her following words.

“Greetings… เอ็ด.”

Was the problem with her head being down like that, as she looked around like she was avoiding me and trying to hide her embarrassment just a little?

In the first place, emotions were contagious. No matter how much I thought about it, it didn’t seem like it would be a big deal. But seeing her twist her body around like that embarrassed all… It was inevitable that I would also start to be affected.

“Ah, yes…”

After saying that, silence.

If anyone were to ask me what there was to be so embarrassed about, I could only tilt my head, unable to answer… Because the reason my voice wasn’t working well wasn’t something one could identify easily.

Above all else, although she had only changed her words slightly, the sense of distance between us and our relationship with each other started to be objectified.

We were friendly with each other and had a special relationship, so I didn’t realize it, but… When we looked back at our relationship after taking a step away from each other, we are acting like completely different people.

เยนิกา was always smiling brightly and acting friendly, but from an objective point of view, she wasn’t someone that you could easily approach.

To say that she was beautiful was the least you could do, but in fact that was only a secondary issue.

She was one of the most talented people in school, one that could even handle high-ranking spirits casually. She was easily recognized as being one of the most talented and was bound to do something great after she graduated.

The reason I had forgotten about that fact was because our relationship had become too comfortable, like family. Looking past the fact that she was always acting so friendly, a sudden strange realization came from an unfamiliar feeling that made me aware of just who she was again.

After exchanging only a few words with each other, we had been quiet for nearly three whole minutes.

“I… Uhm…”

Since I had been cooperating with her thus far, maybe she felt that she had to say something else. So, เยนิกา tried to raise her voice and…

“N-Nevermind…”

Once again, that small, quiet voice of hers returned as she lowered her head, looking down… I couldn’t help but also be nervous seeing her like that. Although we had talked with each other time and time again, I couldn’t quite understand why เยนิกา was suddenly so nervous just because we were speaking politely to each other.

I then started to hold my breath. I didn’t know why, but it felt like we were fighting each other in a game of who could hold their breath the longest.

It would be great if she would just let out a heavy exhale, and laugh bashfully before saying we should end things there… then we would talk about how it was already so late. We would put away the dishes, and she heads back to the dormitory after briefly talking about tomorrow’s schedule…

How long would she hold her breath? That crazy awkward atmosphere between us wasn’t going away.

By the time I realized it, the flow of emotions had already crossed the line. เยนิกา’s face was clearly as red as an apple, and she held her knees together with her head lowered.

เยนิกา suggested it herself, but she had no idea it would be that difficult. And after trying it out, the emotional gap was so much deeper than she had thought. She had no idea how to fix it now. She was like a novice driver that couldn’t figure out the position of the brake.

So, I decided that it was only right for me to clench my teeth and make the first strike. Then,

“Ahh, look at that! It’s almost curfew.”

เยนิกา quickly stood up, kicking the floor and putting on her shawl with the pretty embroidered cosmos on, before picking up her oak wand.

“Ah… Uhm… So…”

Then she chose the worst option: running away. In other words, ‘to keep the situation as it was.’

“Th-Thank you for today. S-See you tomorrow.”

Grasping the wand with both hands, she bowed her head before saying farewell as she quickly ran through the grass.

With that, เยนิกา disappeared from sight in an instant.

“……”

A camp with only the sound of the buzzing bugs in the grass.

After sitting alone at the campfire for a while… I wiped my face.

“Talking politely like this… Are we going to continue it…?”

A cold sweat ran down my back.
