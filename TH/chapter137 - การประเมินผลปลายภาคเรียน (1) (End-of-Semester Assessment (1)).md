By the time ทาเนีย returned to หอพักโอฟีเลียส, it was already dark.

The school uniform she was neatly dressed in that the morning was messy all over. Her thick and beautiful blonde hair had also become a mess, with hair sticking out here and there.

ทาเนีย trudged down the hallway with sunken eyes as she handed the maid her outer coat and luggage.

“You look rather tired.”

The maid spoke to her concerned voice.

ทาเนีย looked as if she was going to loudly growl like a zombie at any moment… However, she simply shook her head with her exhausted body slouching.

“No, I’m fine.”

It had been a while since she had last seen หอพักโอฟีเลียส during the day.

That was because she repeated the same schedule every day. She would leave the dormitory at dawn and wouldn’t return until late at night. However, it was rather rewarding for her, and the work she took care of wasn’t all that difficult.

The gazes of respect and the envy the students had towards ทาเนีย was satisfying. That was because ทาเนีย was the type of person that had a strong desire for honor and respect.

However, in that head of hers there were still a lot of things bothering her.

Her biggest concern was the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้ and the power they had, as they had recently been trying to cross the line. Not just the student council, but also the school had been showing great concern towards them.

Recently, they had raised the market price by sweeping up all the textbooks.

It might have seemed like a simple goal of making a profit by buying them when they were cheap and selling them slowly when they were expensive… But considering เอลเต้’s financial power, one could also think that they are going to take things one step further.

For example, if they didn’t sell back the textbooks, school supplies, stationery, and educational magic engineering equipment, then… The start of the school term might end up facing a major setback. That was because many students would be unable to attend any classes without any tools.

The สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้ was already in charge of all the revenue taking place on เกาะอาเคน alone. So, if they gained a larger influence by creating such hostage situations in the market, it would become a major headache for the school.

She hoped that it was all just a coincidence, but things didn’t always end up the way you wanted them to.

It would be great if the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้ could remain good business partners with ซิลเวเนีย in the long term while maintaining good business ethics.

However, with the power balance slowly starting to skew towards one side… The school was starting to tremble nervously.

If they did end up crossing the line, the school would try to secure additional distribution channels outside of the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้.

And that wouldn’t be a very pleasant situation for the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้. That was because the monopoly they currently had would end up being shaken.

“Ah, my head… It hurts…”

As ทาเนีย stumbled through the fancy hallways, she pressed her hands against her temples.

The school revealed that they were thinking of establishing a plan for opening a new distribution channel in advance.

They said they were planning to release an announcement in the commercial city of โอลเด็ค, but for now it was still only being discussed internally.

For the school, it was much more beneficial and safer for them to create various distribution channels. Of course, that was not possible without having the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้ voice their displeasure.

There was a reason that they alone had been in charge of everything in เกาะอาเคน for so long. That was because the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้ had calculated the amount of investment that would be returned to them in the distant future.

If ซิลเวเนีย were to suddenly become larger and find another business partner in an attempt to take away from the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้’s profits as a monopoly, they wouldn’t just stand still.

That was why they needed someone in the middle to coordinate it all. A person that understood ทาเนีย and the school’s situation but could speak up against the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้ on their own.

“For now… I should just go into my room and lay down for a bit.”

It had been an incredibly busy day for her. For now, ทาเนีย just wanted to rest.

She walked down the hallway, planning to go back to her room quickly, when—

“……”

She suddenly turned around. ทาเนีย wasn’t the only one staggering along.

She hadn’t sensed anyone else’s presence nearby, so ทาเนีย trembled.

However, it was possible. After all, the girl’s body was quite small compared to the average person. She was also as light as a feather, as she had all sorts of weight-lightening magic applied to her body.

The only reason why her presence was noticeable was because of her large witch hat, which was the size of her upper body. The hat, which was too large for the girl, was this girl’s symbol.

“L-ลูซี่…?”

ลูซี่ also trembled in surprise. In the first place, she was a girl that wasn’t at all interested in the people around her. However, as soon as she saw ทาเนีย, she was startled. She then swung her body around as she clung onto a window in the hallway, hiding her face.

After making sure that the person talking to her was ทาเนีย, she relaxed her body—as if she were reassured.

ลูซี่ didn’t judge people on their appearance alone. She was sharp when it came to judging a person’s mood and other behavioral characteristics. She might always appear to look like a lazy and disinterested person, but thanks to her senses, she was keenly aware of her surroundings.

At first, when she sensed there was someone there she thought that it was someone other than ทาเนีย.

เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์.

Naturally, her bright blonde hair was similar to เอ็ด’s. Since they were siblings born of the same house, that was inevitable.

“H-Hello…”

ทาเนีย greeted her awkwardly. It was impossible to not be awkward around her.

At the beginning of the semester, ลูซี่ lost control of herself and became furious, trying to catch and kill ทาเนีย.

However, after all the misunderstandings were cleared up, เอ็ด had brought ลูซี่ to apologize to ทาเนีย… But there was still a strange awkwardness between them.

Although they still maintained an awkward relationship, ลูซี่ did live in the room right next to ทาเนีย’s.

Since they saw each other whenever they came and went from their rooms, it was a bit uncomfortable for the both of them.

Neither ลูซี่ nor ทาเนีย were in a great situation because of that.

“I-I…”

ลูซี่ relaxed her body as she exhaled deeply. ทาเนีย was thinking about what she should say to ลูซี่ when—

“I’m sorry about last time.”

ลูซี่ was the one that apologized first. And she did it in a pretty relaxed manner.

“I misunderstood.”

“I-I’s alright. You already apologized to me, you don’t have to do it again…”

ทาเนีย shook her head, wondering how she should tear down the awkward atmosphere between them.

Suddenly, she noticed that ลูซี่’s clothes were rather dirty.

“I see that you just came back from taking a nap somewhere. These days, the weather is quite nice to spend your time outdoors. The temperature is also quite good…”

It was just a casual topic to continue their conversation. Although they were just words of courtesy, ลูซี่ listened carefully, nodding her head.

“Do you still go to my brother’s camp often? I heard that the two of you were close.”

“I don’t really go these days.”

“H-Huh…? Is that so…? Wh-Why…?”

Did the two of them fight? ทาเนีย didn’t have the slightest idea.

It seemed like เอ็ด, who was rather diligent, and ลูซี่, who was lazy, were complete opposites. However, there was still one thing that they had in common that everyone agreed on.

Neither of them were all that interested in the people around them.

Unless they needed to become involved with them, they would never act courteously to try and get close to someone first.

Because of that temperament, เอ็ด and ลูซี่ both didn’t have particularly high expectations of each other.

Maybe it was just a relationship between a man that lived and took care of his cabin and a girl who occasionally came to visit to take a nap.

ทาเนีย had thought of things like that, but became puzzled upon seeing ลูซี่’s subtly awkward reaction.

“Since I took a nap somewhere else… I look a bit… messy.”

“…What?”

“My skirt is dirty, I’m covered in dust, and my hair is a mess…”

Just hearing the conversation alone, you wouldn’t think there was anything strange about it. However, if you were to think about who was talking… those were some unbelievable words coming out of her mouth.

ทาเนีย was a bit shocked, but the maid next to her, holding onto her luggage and coat, also widened her eyes to the point that they looked like they might pop out.

She wasn’t just anyone. She was ลูซี่ เมย์ริล, and was caring about her appearance right…

If it weren’t for the maids, ลูซี่ เมย์ริล wouldn’t even tie her hair back. She would only wear loose fitted clothes… The maids never wanted that unsightly appearance of hers to be shown in front of anyone else.

The maid next to ทาเนีย suddenly started to doubt that the girl was truly ลูซี่ เมย์ริล.

Was she caring about how other people looked at her? ลูซี่ เมย์ริล?

Only then did ทาเนีย and the maid realize it. ลูซี่ เมย์ริล was finally becoming a girl.

It was still just the beginning. She was still only at a level where she didn’t want to show an unsightly appearance of herself to others.

She still had the same old egocentric and lazy temperament. She didn’t have any girly hobbies and she still took a nap in the middle of nowhere as if it were her job.

Wearing a school uniform that didn’t fit her, disappearing like a ghost here and there throughout the school. She hadn’t changed at all. After all, human nature was one that changed quite easily.

However, even massive flames started from weak embers.

Originally, becoming more girly like that wasn’t something that just passed by. Instead, it would slowly start to grow.

ทาเนีย closed her eyes, imagining it.

ลูซี่ เมย์ริล taking off her witch hat, neatly tidying up her pretty white hair, and smiling in a clean, neat, and cute dress.

Suddenly, a chill ran down her spine. She thought that was all a bit too far-fetched.

“Maybe tomorrow I will go to the camp…”

“Ah, is that so…?”

“Yeah. Since I haven’t gone in a while… I kind of want to go…”

Like a stray cat, she pressed down on her hat tightly, acting shy. It seemed as if that appearance of hers was a fatal blow to the maid.

She clutched her heart as she jumped forward, grabbing ลูซี่’s hand.

“D-Don’t worry, คุณหนูลูซี่. Tomorrow I’ll use all the knowledge I gained while working as a Senior Maid to make sure to dress you up beautifully. You’re already beautiful in the first place, so don’t you worry one bit…!”

“……”

Seeing the maid suddenly cheer on ลูซี่ with all her heart was a very strange and abrupt thing to witness.

ลูซี่ nodded, looking a little nervous.

ทาเนีย went into her room after saying goodbye to ลูซี่. The private rooms of หอพักโอฟีเลียส, which were as magnificent as one would expect, looked more like the interior of a palace than a regular student dorm.

She took off her school uniform before she changed into comfortable clothes. She then went over to the table, sitting down. She looked down, seeing that there was a letter on the table for some reason.

After the maids finished cleaning, they sometimes left letters addressed to the students out on the table in their private rooms. It was quite convenient to not need to go out and personally pick up their mail.

However, ever since she became the student council president, most of the letters she received came through the student council.

Most of them were about public affairs, as she didn’t want to look through work-related documents when she was in the dormitory.

So, what was the purpose of the letter sent through the dormitory…? It was a letter sent from home.

“This is father’s… wax seal…”

It’s been a while since she has exchanged letters with เครปิน โรสเทย์เลอร์. She had already warned him about it in advance.

When she became the student council president, she would become so busy that she wouldn’t have the time to update him on her life. So, she told him to be understanding of her situation.

เครปิน was really proud of his daughter, who had become the student council president. And it didn’t seem as if he was all that interested in whether or not she was adapting well to her school life.

In the first place, เครปิน โรสเทย์เลอร์ already has his hands full taking care of the affairs of the Imperial Family. There was no room to pay any attention to the affairs of เกาะอาเคน, which was located at the southernmost tip of the continent.

That was why she thought there weren’t going to be any letters from him any time soon. ทาเนีย herself was really busy.

“This is the first letter in such a long time…”

ทาเนีย opened it up as she flipped through the various words of courtesy written by เครปิน โรสเทย์เลอร์.

What was it like to become the student council president, whether or not she was adjusting to her school life, if she had made any friends, what kind of people were the children of the noble and influential families, and whether the plan to purchase the Sage’s Seal was starting to come together.

Reading through similar questions, ทาเนีย relaxed herself as she finished reading through it all.

However, what was written at the end made ทาเนีย’s eyes widen.

It was completely unbelievable. ทาเนีย had to quietly read it aloud once more.

“Since it’s right before your first break… I’ve come to want to see your face again. I heard that many students go home and rest over the break, so ทาเนีย, please come back home and rest for a bit before returning to ซิลเวเนีย. If possible…”

ทาเนีย put the letter down leaning against the back of her chair.

“If possible… With เอ็ด, too…”

What was written at the end was shocking.

*I know that เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์ is still alive.*

*However, if his reputation within the school is good, his magic skill have improved, and he’s sincere about things… Then, as the head of house, I want to accept เอ็ด back into ตระกูลโรสเทย์เลอร์.*

*I will no longer bring up his crime of insulting the princess. No matter how bad of a crime he committed, I want to give him one last chance—as both the head of house, and his father.*

*So, pass on this message to เอ็ด.*

*After losing Arwen, I don’t want history to repeat itself again.*

So, if you wish, the doors of ตระกูลโรสเทย์เลอร์ are still open for you.

I don’t want you to end your life as someone excommunicated from a noble family.

Please come back to the house this break, share with me the stories you have, and let’s seriously discuss the direction your life is taking.
