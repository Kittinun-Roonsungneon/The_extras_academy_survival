“คฤหาสน์โรสเทย์เลอร์… It’s been five years, Emperor.”

The dispute over the succession of the emperor’s throne, which burned like a flame, seemed to take shape to some extent as the years passed.

One day, Prince Lindon suddenly gave up his right to the throne. And thus, a war of nerves was born that continued for a long time.

The three princesses of จักรพรรดิโครเอล fought amongst each other to some extent in a power struggle… but now, that too was becoming a thing of the past.

The third princess—เพเนีย, who received the most support—was off at เกาะอาเคน, which was in the southernmost corner of the continent. The second princess—เพอร์ซิกา—was also buried away in the Imperial Library.

Was she being cautious of the disloyal servants around her, who approached her with only a lust for power? Or was she holding her breath in preparation for a full-fledged battle?

The reason was unknown, but at the very least, it was good news for the first princess, เซลาห์.

“If it’s an order, I should go. As ตระกูลโรสเทย์เลอร์ is a prestigious family that contributes greatly to the Imperial Family, it is necessary to personally introduce myself to them.”

She was a girl that had an unknown chill around her, much like the calm winter air.

She was neither arrogant nor disruptive. The only thing around her was a delicate elegance, like a piece of ice.

Even her hair had a blue-ish glow, like a snowflake covered with thin ice.

In the center of the audience chamber, which was massive compared to the size of the girl, the ruler of the โครเอล empire that was also known as a legendary warrior, โครเอล, was seated on his throne.

An old-fashioned silk carpet stretched out from his throne, all the way to เซลาห์’s feet.

The imperial guards stood solemnly with their spears erect, not moving one bit. Numerous aides that gathered to give advice to the emperor were also bowing their heads.

“I will make the preparations and leave on time. But…”

จักรพรรดิโครเอล had ordered First เจ้าหญิงเซลาห์ to visit the คฤหาสน์โรสเทย์เลอร์.

It seemed as if he had already spoken with his closest confidant, เครปิน โรสเทย์เลอร์, about it already.

“It is a shame that I won’t be able to see เพเนีย because the timing doesn’t match.”

เซลาห์, the Princess of Frost, spoke as she bowed her head.

There was a strange silence among the closest aides that sat on both sides of จักรพรรดิโครเอล. That was because her words held many meanings.

It had been well over a year since the Benevolent เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย เอเลียส โครเอล had left the imperial household to study.

No matter how much support she received, being away from the house meant that her power was weak.

However, since เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย was returning for the break, if the forces supporting เพเนีย used that time as an opportunity to gather and nurture their loyalty, things wouldn’t be great for เซลาห์.

เซลาห์ wanted to stay in the imperial palace to watch over the situation. So, it wasn’t great that she was being sent to the คฤหาสน์โรสเทย์เลอร์, which was far away, while เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย returned.

เพเนีย said that she didn’t desire power, but เซลาห์ didn’t believe her.

That was why hearing จักรพรรดิโครเอล’s orders to leave the imperial household while เพเนีย returned home wasn’t very pleasing. Of course, เซลาห์ didn’t have the right to refuse.

“I understand your desire to see เพเนีย after such a long time, but going to ตระกูลโรสเทย์เลอร์ is a much bigger opportunity for you.”

Was it possible that he was forcibly separating her from เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย? เซลาห์ held such thoughts, but จักรพรรดิโครเอล would never say such a thing out loud.

“เซลาห์, you must also know the importance of the social gathering held at the คฤหาสน์โรสเทย์เลอร์.”

เซลาห์ nodded her head.

The head of ตระกูลโรสเทย์เลอร์, เครปิน โรสเทย์เลอร์, didn’t enjoy such social gatherings.

However, as he was labeled the most powerful man on the continent, it was necessary for him to get along well with many nobles.

That was why, when the rare social gathering was held, it was rather lavish.

It was a golden opportunity for the smaller aristocrats on the outskirts. It was a rare event, one where all sorts of people from powerful and noble families gathered in one place.

Originally, the imperial family had a custom where they would send one of the closest aides in their place. However, that year they were going to raise the status of the one they sent, and instead send the first princess.

There had long been a deep exchange between the โครเอล and โรสเทย์เลอร์ families. So, that was a way to show their connection.

The impending social gathering was rather an opportunity.

In order to become the emperor, one would need to thoroughly learn how to handle ตระกูลโรสเทย์เลอร์ to properly use them. Thus, it was necessary to have a deeper understanding of them and interact with the members of the family.

But it wouldn’t end there.

The massive social gathering was held over a period of five days to easily accommodate the busy schedules of all the participants.

Looking at the list of names invited, there were plenty of major individuals that moved the empire. Every major person she could think of was on that list.

Margrave Zahul, who alone managed the largest granary in the southernmost part of the empire.

The investor Roland, who was responsible for the practical money flow of the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้.

Evian Nortondale, who was head of House Nortondale, which was one of the most prestigious swordsman households.

Valvern, who was known as an incredible innovator and considered a ‘father’ to all the alchemists in Krett, the land of alchemy.

นักบุญหญิงคลาริซ, who was praised as a noble messenger of God, standing at the top of the ศาสนานิกายเทลอส Group.

Sinir Bloomriver, who was the head of House Bloomriver, famous for its magic and known as the ‘witch’s house’.

Commander Magnus Callamore, who was famous for commanding the northern frontier’s troops and killing the largest number of non-humans.

And then there was the first princess of the จักรวรรดิโครเอล, เซลาห์, who had emerged as a powerful individual and the current runner-up as the next emperor.

Of course, as it is taking place in the โรสเทย์เลอร์ Household, all the members of ตระกูลโรสเทย์เลอร์ will be there.

The noble เครปิน โรสเทย์เลอร์, his successor, ทาเนีย โรสเทย์เลอร์.

Plus, เครปิน had said that he was planning on reinstating his precious son, เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์, during the event.

Wasn’t that the same son that he himself had excommunicated for slandering the noble and benevolent เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย? Even though he said that he would accept him as a member of the family again, his real intentions were unknown.

Well… right now, the most likely successor is still the youngest, ทาเนีย โรสเทย์เลอร์.

เซลาห์ left the room as the knights escorted her.

As she traversed through the imperial gardens, which were beautifully well kept and rather large, she fell into deep thought.

Even though many people would be attending, it was impossible to personally meet with everyone.

She would try her hardest to get to know all of them as much as she could, but… she still needed to set priorities.

Strength, financial power, and even religious support.

She narrowed her list down to the Northern Corps Commander Magnus Callamore, the investor with connections to the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้, Roland, and นักบุญหญิงคลาริซ from the religious group.

Plus, it was necessary to get to know the members of the โรสเทย์เลอร์ Household once more. She didn’t have any previous meetings with ทาเนีย โรสเทย์เลอร์, who was currently the heir, so she planned to use that time to get close to her.

“Hmm…”

She was thinking about เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์, who was meant to be reinstated.

She had no way of knowing what เครปิน โรสเทย์เลอร์ was thinking. เซลาห์ heard a rumor long ago that เอ็ด was excommunicated and ruined, with no support from his family.

He was someone that had lived on the ground as a fallen nobleman for nearly two years now.

He had lost all of his dignity and authority. But more than anything, he must have experienced just how hellish life was without the glory of his family’s name.

Rather than trying to fight for the position of heir, such people would instead focus on clinging to their families, afraid of being kicked out.

Since he had been at the bottom for several years, his position within the family would be ambiguous. He also wouldn’t have had many chances to interact with influential individuals. It was obvious that he would try and turn things around during the social gathering.

Still, if she saw potential in him, she was open to the idea of investing early. However… given how much time had passed, she didn’t know if it was worth investing in him. To put it bluntly, he was someone of minor importance to her.

“เจ้าหญิงเซลาห์. I came to tell you that we will start preparing for your trip to ตระกูลโรสเทย์เลอร์.”

“Please do so.”

After responding to the guards, เซลาห์ entered the palace gates.

No matter what, the person that เซลาห์ was thinking about the most at that moment was เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย.

She had no idea what เพเนีย would do when she returned to the imperial palace during vacation. After all, during that period เซลาห์ herself would be vacant from her seat.

Feeling a bit uneasy, เซลาห์ decided to direct her closest aides to keep her mind focused.

* * *

“We were waiting for you. Come inside.”

The captain of the escort team of เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย, Claire, bowed her head.

I thanked her as I entered through the front door of the royal residence. ทาเนีย, who timidly followed behind me, looked around.

“This seems like an entirely different world, brother.”

ทาเนีย, who entered the royal residence, looked around as she quickly cleared her throat in fear that she may have looked undignified.

The most luxurious and expensive facility on เกาะอาเคน was หอพักโอฟีเลียส. However, if you were to simply look at the cost of expenses, then the royal residence would be next.

There was a difference in size.

หอพักโอฟีเลียส was a large dormitory that was made for students of various aristocratic families.

The royal residence was less than half the size of หอพักโอฟีเลียส, but still it was only built for one person. It was an unbelievable level of luxury.

It took a while just to enter the heavily guarded main entrance, cross through the garden, and then enter the mansion-like residence.

Only after traveling through the clean and luxurious hallways for some time was I finally able to see เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย’s reception room.

“W-Wait a moment…”

Before ทาเนีย entered the room, she quickly straightened her hair as she tidied up her outfit.

ทาเนีย had already met เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย quite a few times. However, it was the first time she had officially visited her since becoming the student council president.

The morning before, ทาเนีย wrote a letter to the royal residence.

Summer vacation was starting, and the โรสเทย์เลอร์ siblings were going back home. However, before that, there was something they wanted to ask of เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย. That was the gist of the letter.

เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย wasn’t an easy person to meet. However, whether it was because ทาเนีย was the student council president or if she had just wanted to meet with us after seeing our names, she accepted surprisingly readily.

Thus, the two of us had gone to the royal residence early that morning.

When the door opened up, I saw เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย sitting alone on a sofa that looked quite expensive even from a glance.

Her lace dress also exuded a noble atmosphere. Her hair fell down past her shoulders.

The size of the sofa was much too large for her body.

She looked noble, but at the same time, rather lonely.

“Hello, เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย. Thank you for allowing us an audience.”

After I greeted her, ทาเนีย quickly followed suit, bowing her head.

เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย lowered her gaze as she nodded. She then looked at the sofa across from her. ทาเนีย and I went to sit on it without saying a word.

“You look healthy, เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์. And you as well, Miss ทาเนีย.”

“Yes. Have you been doing well yourself, Princess?”

“……”

เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย didn’t answer for a moment as she lowered her gaze once more.

“It’s the same as always.”

The butler of the residence politely walked up to me, putting down some tea. ทาเนีย quickly took a sip.

“เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย. The reason we came here today is because…”

“You must be in need of my authority.”

เพเนีย jumped straight to the point without hesitation.

Whether it was because of how ทาเนีย and I looked in her eyes, or if it was because of her great insight…

I didn’t know the answer to that, but at the very least, she didn’t appear hostile.

Rather, เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย’s tone of voice was rather gentle.

“เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์, it was my fault that you were excommunicated. Thus, with my approval, you will have an easier time being reinstated.”

It felt as if she were being rather straightforward.

Either way, since I had to be reinstated, she already knew what we came for.

Going back to the โรสเทย์เลอร์ Household is a major gamble for me. It was something that required a lot of preparation.

In terms of strength, we had ลูซี่, so that wasn’t a problem. Still, not everything could be solved with force alone.

Among nobility, ultimately most problems were resolved through ‘authority’.

Gaining recognition from someone with high authority or an influential individual made it difficult to put your hands on them.

“Come to think of it, it’s been a long time since I sent my regards to the head of ตระกูลโรสเทย์เลอร์. เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์, could you deliver my handwritten letter to เครปิน โรสเทย์เลอร์ on my behalf?”

เพเนีย didn’t say much. She had already prepared the letter in advance.

A servant slowly approached me as he gave me the personal letter of เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย… It had a luxurious gold frame around it and the seal of the โครเอล Imperial Family was also attached.

A letter from the imperial family wasn’t something that could just be delivered by anyone. Thus, the meaning of that single paged letter was much heavier than you would think.

However, the most important point was that it was not sealed with wax.

The fact that it wasn’t sealed implied that the sender fully trusted the one delivering the letter. It showed that she had complete trust and belief that I wouldn’t do anything inappropriate with it.

Historically, it was a way to introduce someone that you trusted. It was a needlessly complicated piece of etiquette, but the royal family had always been like that.

The fact was, I was recognized by เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย and fully entrusted with her authority. The letter, by its very existence, granted a special authority that prevented me, as the messenger, from being easily messed with.

“There is no need for me to write a letter granting you authority. That letter alone should be enough for you to return in glory.”

“I had no idea you would help me so easily. I… was ready to negotiate with you more if needed.”

“……”

เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย didn’t respond to that.

She was a person that had a skeptical view of ตระกูลโรสเทย์เลอร์. She also intended to reveal the hidden internal affairs of ตระกูลโรสเทย์เลอร์.

However, before เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย started to take on such a troublesome task, she was willingly cooperating with us.

But she wasn’t saying anything else. After I spoke, there was a long moment of silence. So much so I started to turn red with embarrassment.

เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย was staring at me for a long period of time.

Her gaze was heavy, so I tried to find another topic to talk about… But it was of no use.

* * *

“The carriage sent from คฤหาสน์โรสเทย์เลอร์ will arrive in about two days.”

After saying that, ทาเนีย went back to หอพักโอฟีเลียส. Now it was time to really go back home.

While I was back in the mansion, I wouldn’t be able to manage the camp. That was why it was necessary to take several measures to ensure that it was alright for me to be absent long-term.

I needed to make a lock for the door of my cabin to shut it tight. I also needed to organize all of my tools that were scattered outside.

I still needed to retrieve all the traps I had set up in the forest, clean them up, and put them in storage. All of the food I had, aside from what I could store for a long time, needed to be disposed of.

After thinking about all the work that I needed to finish that day, I returned to the camp.

“E-เอ็ด. You’re heeeeeere~!”

เยนิกา, who sat by the fireplace, looked at me with a strange tone in her voice as she spoke.

Even if she was trying to raise the tone of her voice on purpose and pretend to be fine, it was clear that she was nervous.

“Look at this. เบลล์ gave us precious herbs, and I used them in the curry~! I tried it earlier, and~… It was really good~!”

“……”

As I stared at เยนิกา’s face, she started to shift her feet and sweat profusely.

“Why… are you looking at me like that…?”

Seeing her hiccup and stumble on her words was very much like herself.

As if I was staring a hole through her head, เยนิกา had to glance away for a moment.

“……”

“Are you thinking about something useless again…?”

“No, but…”

Since the end of the exams, เยนิกา has been acting like that.

She put her chin on her knee as she rested it. She always did that when she was depressed.

“I… Thought I knew you well, but. Sometimes I feel like I don’t know a thing…”

“About me?”

I took off my shoes and poured out the dirt that was inside.

“I’m sure of it though. There isn’t anyone in this school as close to me as you.”

“Well, maybe physically our bodies might be close, but… I have no idea what you are thinking or how you live.”

เยนิกา took a deep breath. I wondered what was with her, but suddenly something came to mind.

“Is this about you giving up the top student seat? You think that was rude to me?”

“Hiccup…! No! No! No! No! No!”

“I’m sure of it now, seeing as you had to say it five times.”

As I brushed off my shoes, the dirt rose up. I frowned as I waved my hand to get it out of my face. Either way, living in the forest, whenever you walk around, you’re bound to get your clothes dirty quickly.

“There’s no such thing as a person that does everything right the first time. That’s how we learn.”

“…Still, เอ็ด… That’s completely different…”

“……?”

“เอ็ด, can you please forgive me this one time? Whether it was an accident… or a mistake I did on purpose…”

When I looked towards เยนิกา with an expression that said that was absurd, she quickly waved her arms in the air. Seeing an expression that said she was the sorriest person in the entire world on her face, I was the one feeling sorry for her.

“D-Did I just make another mistake with what I just said?”

“What thing did you do that was so wrong?”

เยนิกา closed her eyes tightly as she confessed.

“I-I also think my head is going to explode. I just keep having these bad thoughts…”

“Bad thoughts?”

“…เอ็ด. I thought that you might hate me.”

Again, I was left speechless.

เยนิกา was a good-natured and kind person, but sometimes that attitude of hers could be rude to others.

The nicer and kinder you were, the rarer the experience of being hated by others was. So, for her, it seemed like she might never have been hated by another before.

That was why เยนิกา was struggling so much. She was afraid that I might end up hating her.

“You don’t have to worry.”

“……”

“Even if you were a little rude, do you think I would end up hating you so easily? You are a special person to me.”

เยนิกา took a short breath as she looked up into my eyes. Looking at the tears welling up in the corners of her eyes, it seemed that she was worrying quite a bit.

Looking at that, didn’t it seem like I always did bad things to her? I always tried my best to treat her well though.

“Anyway… I have something to ask you. Are you planning on going back home this break?”

“Hm?”

“Do you think you can accept this simple request?”

* * *

Two days later, a luxurious carriage crossed สะพานเมกเซส.

I waited by the front entrance with my luggage, already packed. I also took ทาเนีย’s luggage as well.

“They sent quite a large carriage. I guess since we will spend quite some time inside it, it’s better that it’s larger and more comfortable.”

“Did you sleep well last night?”

“No. I was planning to sleep in the carriage, so I did some work instead. You look rather tired yourself, brother.”

“I’m also exhausted after having taken care of some work at the camp.”

“I see. Uhm… But…”

ทาเนีย looked at the carriage crossing สะพานเมกเซส as she swung towards me and whispered.

“The atmosphere is already so scary. Will it be like this the entire time in the carriage…?”

I turned my head and looked behind me at the two girls standing side by side.

One girl had pink hair and wore a white blouse, a blue flared skirt, and a brown shawl.

The other girl was smaller, with white hair hanging over her loosely fitted shirt and skirt.

It went without saying, but it was เยนิกา เฟลเลเวอร์ and ลูซี่ เมย์ริล.

The two looked at each other as if puzzled… They were both making faces as if wondering ‘why are you here?’

…And that was how things happened.
