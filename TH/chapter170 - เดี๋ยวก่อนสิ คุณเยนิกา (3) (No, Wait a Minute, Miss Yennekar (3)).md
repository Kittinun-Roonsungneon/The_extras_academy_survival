Until she smelt the morning air, it didn’t feel like she had properly started her day.

เบลล์ มายาร์, who had been living in the same pattern for the past ten years, was used to waking up with the early morning air before the sun even rose.

The chirping of sparrows was heard throughout the rose garden of หอพักโอฟีเลียส.

There was still quite some time left until morning would come, but เบลล์ มายาร์ was already wearing her maid outfit as she crossed the hallway on the first floor of หอพักโอฟีเลียส.

The hardworking senior maids had already opened all of the windows in the hallway during the early dawn. It was very important to ventilate the hallway with the fresh morning air.

She would greet the other maids who were cleaning in the morning, sign the logbooks of the maids who had been working throughout the night, and go to inspect the various ingredients that were delivered to หอพักโอฟีเลียส’s cafeteria.

The inspection itself was a job that the senior maids took care of, but เบลล์ มายาร์, the head maid, had to go there to make sure that everything was going well.

Because of the strict food policy at หอพักโอฟีเลียส, about 15% of all the groceries that came in from the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้ were rejected on the spot every day.

Vegetables that had gone a little bad as they were being transported, or livestock products that weren’t fresh, wouldn’t be paid for.

You might think that they were being too unreasonable from the perspective of the supplier, but in the first place, the orders placed at หอพักโอฟีเลียส were all for the highest-quality ingredients. They didn’t complain about their large orders in the first place.

The profits generated by major business partners such as หอพักโอฟีเลียส were so massive that the amount discarded during inspection was nothing to fret over.

However, as for the ingredients that didn’t pass inspection, it didn’t mean that they were returned to the company.

In the first place, if they brought it all the way back to โอลเด็ค—the freshness of the ingredients would greatly worsen. Also, the costs for transporting them were too high.

Thus, because there was little to no benefit in doing so, it was better for them to use their time and funds to do something else, rather than bringing it back to โอลเด็ค.

In other words, ingredients that did not pass inspection were discarded at หอพักโอฟีเลียส. Normally, the delivery workers or the maids on duty took a few.

As for the dairy products and spices, เบลล์ มายาร์ also… thought of grabbing them and bringing them back to เอ็ด’s camp, as they were difficult to obtain.

It was going to be discarded anyway, so it wasn’t really a crime.

“Here is the checklist, head maid. By the way… that is…”

In any case, the checklist needed to be approved by the head maid.

The only difference was whether the senior maid would go to visit the head maid with the documents or if เบลล์ มายาร์ was already at the location to sign it.

เบลล์, who accepted the checklist, tilted her head for a moment while looking at the prices of the various ingredients written on it.

“Food prices have gone up a lot. Compared to before break… It’s definitely troubling.”

“I checked the production and distribution of crops, but there were no major issues…”

The senior maid lowered her head as if she were apologetic.

เบลล์ shrugged as she continued to check the price of items on the list. It was definitely an unnatural rise in price.

“I will explain that.”

It was a boy who was at the location as a representative for the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้. His name was ดูน.

He wore a beret and looked rather cunning.

“The price of logistics from โอลเด็ค to เกาะอาเคน has risen overall. In fact, it’s not just ingredients, but the prices of school supplies and magic supplies, especially magic engineering supplies, have risen quite drastically. As a result, other items that need to be delivered have also increased. We have to charge at least this much to make a profit, so it’s something that we can’t help.”

“Was this matter approved by the deputy director, ลอร์เทล?”

“Yes, of course~”

เบลล์ มายาร์ looked at the cunning-looking boy as she thought things over for a bit.

In any case, her job was to look over the documents, not to make a political judgment about their decision.

The list of prices presented by the สมาคมการค้าเอลเต้ showed a consistent upwards trend for all of their items.

“I understand.”

เบลล์ nodded as she signed the checklist.

Whack, Whack, Whack!

When เบลล์ มายาร์ arrived at เอ็ด’s camp, there was a periodic sound, as if someone were hitting a tree.

Nearing the river, she could clearly tell that the noise was เอ็ด chopping wood.

His condition hadn’t fully healed, but he had already started working.

เบลล์ gripped the wooden basket full of various ingredients as she entered the camp.

The campfire was still burning as a familiar-looking suitcase was lying next to it.

The wooden luggage made from birch wood of the Pulan region belonged to เยนิกา เฟลเลเวอร์. Judging by the wand lying next to it, it seemed that เยนิกา เฟลเลเวอร์ had returned last night.

Summer break was slowly coming to an end, and it was already time to start preparing for the next semester, so it wasn’t that strange for her to already be back at school.

“Hmm.”

เบลล์ took a short breath as she looked at เยนิกา’s cabin.

Before going over to tell เอ็ด to stop working so hard, she wanted to say hello to เยนิกา. Putting the basket full of ingredients on เอ็ด’s workbench, she went to เยนิกา’s cabin, knocking on the door.

Knock, knock

Creak

As soon as she knocked on the door, the door opened on its own.

เบลล์ tilted her head as she looked inside. There, she saw เยนิกา lying on the bed, buried like a corpse with her head covered by her pillow.

“Lady เยนิกา… ?”

“Oh, เบลล์…! It’s you, เบลล์…! I-I was surprised because I thought เอ็ด had come in!”

“Ah, I apologize…”

“Oh, no… There’s no reason for you to apologize…”

เบลล์ was confused, but she didn’t let it show. As always, she succeeded in responding calmly and quietly.

“I see you have come back. Are you sick? Your complexion is a bit… it doesn’t look great…”

“Oh, uh… I-It’s because I didn’t sleep well.”

“It’s been a while since you last slept in this bed, so that makes sense. You look very tired.”

“Yeah, ah, no… well… I just had a bunch of thoughts, that’s why.”

“A bunch of thoughts…?”

“Yeah, they were just random thoughts! It’s nothing that great and there’s no need to be so curious about it. It was just miscellaneous thoughts!”

เยนิกา grabbed her pillow tightly as she sat up, raising her upper body.

“S-Seeing your face after such a long time really makes me feel as if I’ve come back to ซิลเวเนีย. H-Have you been well?!”

“Are you sure you’re okay? If you are sick, I can give you some medicine.”

“N-No, I’m really fine! I got a little hot last night, but it wasn’t because I was sick or anything! I-It was just that… I just got a little hot for another reason!”

“You got hot, but you aren’t sick? You shouldn’t be too quick to come to that conclusion on your own, Lady เยนิกา.”

“N-No I’m sure of it! I didn’t get hot because I was sick! It’s my body, so I’m sure of it!”

Why would a person’s body heat up without them being sick?

เบลล์ hesitated for a moment, as she couldn’t understand. Then, another possibility came to mind.

The quick-witted เบลล์ quickly realized that เยนิกา’s behavior was unusual.

However, it was still a possibility that was difficult to even guess, so she decided to just test her luck.

“It seems that นายน้อย เอ็ด has been cutting firewood since early in the morning. His health has not fully recovered yet, so if he starts going back to work already, his condition might get worse.”

“I-Is that so…?”

“Yes. So, as his caregiver, I want him to hold back a little… Would you like to come with me to talk to him?”

เยนิกา was living in the same camp as เอ็ด, and she was not the type of person who hated him, either… It was a suggestion that she had no real reason to refuse.

“N-No thanks… I’m a little…”

But เยนิกา was holding her pillow tightly as she swallowed her breath. At that point, เบลล์ มายาร์ was convinced.

Something happened last night.

It was clear from the fact that เยนิกา’s luggage was still outside, as she didn’t’ bring it into her cabin. There was also her strange attitude, as if she was pretending that she was fine.

เยนิกา and เอ็ด, who were reunited after a long time… Who knows what kind of conversation they had while looking at each other with the campfire between them… One’s imagination could run wild.

It was a private matter, and that secrecy deserved to be kept, but… เบลล์ มายาร์ was only human…!

No matter how professional a maid she was, she couldn’t completely control her curiosity…!

Not only that, but it was also เอ็ด’s camp, not หอพักโอฟีเลียส. That meant it was an environment where she could be free from the heavy burden of being the head maid…

เบลล์ มายาร์’s unchanging principle of always acting like a professional slowly started to crack.

In fact, เบลล์ deserved it. Considering her dedication, being curious about something like that wasn’t all that great a crime.

However, it was impossible to ask เยนิกา for the details.

As a veteran, she decided to elicit เยนิกา’s confession with a quick attack.

“…By any chance, did you sleep with นายน้อย เอ็ด last night?”

เบลล์ knew that couldn’t be the case.

“What are you talking about?!!! We didn’t go that far!!!”

With a completely startled expression, เยนิกา raised her voice, frowning.

“…’That far’…?”

“Huh.”

In the end, เยนิกา couldn’t escape from เบลล์ มายาร์’s palm.

Her eyes widened as she tried to belatedly close her mouth. However, the trap had already been set.

“Then… How far did you go…?”

Checkmate.

When I was in good shape, I was able to cut firewood for two hours and be completely fine. But in my current state, I was exhausted just working for thirty minutes.

My body had not yet fully recovered. However, if I started rehabilitation now, I should be fine when the curriculum started at the beginning of the semester.

Wiping away my sweat, I continued looking over my magic skills.

Through various hardships, I was now close to being a master at beginner level magic.

As for Wind Blade and Ignite, there were only a handful of magicians who had more firepower than me.

My magic power efficiency had also fully developed. It had almost reached a point where I could use it as easily as breathing.

Now, I wanted to increase the number of intermediate spells I could use. Advanced magic as well, if possible.

As for intermediate magic, as long as I followed along with my classes, I would likely be able to learn a few. But for advanced magic, it was a completely different story.

Normally, most people were unable to use advanced magic before they graduated.

Even when you became a full-fledged magician, you still had to train for many years to reach the realm of advanced magic.

Students who wielded advanced magic… They were so rare that there were only one or two students who were capable of such a feat in each grade level.

Currently, there were less than ten students who could use Advanced Elemental Magic at สถาบันซิลเวเนีย.

ลูซี่ เมย์ริล, ซิกส์ ไอเฟลสไตน์, ทราเซียนา บลูมริเวอร์, Atalanta, and a few other honor students in fourth year.

It was too greedy to expect student magicians to be capable of using advanced magic. That was why advanced magic wasn’t included in the school’s curriculum. It was a field that was only allowed to some geniuses.

In other words, it was an area that could not be reached through the school’s regular classes… I had to find another way.

“Well, if you’re having a hard time in school or if Assistant ศาสตราจารย์คลีโอห์ is acting like an ass, you can come and tell me about it. Because if there’s one thing that I’m really good at, it’s harassing someone.”

What suddenly came to mind was ศาสตราจารย์คาเลอิด, who I had faced in the final exam.

There weren’t many professors who could teach advanced magic to students. Because advanced magic itself was so complicated and difficult to learn, it wasn’t an easy task to teach it.

And I just happened to be a student assistant in Assistant ศาสตราจารย์คลีโอห์’s lab. So, that meant I can easily meet with Assistant ศาสตราจารย์คลีโอห์’s advisor, ศาสตราจารย์คาเลอิด.

He was constantly drunk and always smoking. He really was the worst of the worst. Without his experience, there would be no way he would be able to make a living. He was the definition of a person who lived life haphazardly.

Because he was a professor with such poor rumors about him going around, no one wanted to learn magic from him.

“Hmm…”

I remember seeing ศาสตราจารย์คาเลอิด several times in the setting book.

The three professors that followed the guardian Obel Forsyth to เกาะอาเคน to teach: Inquirer กลาสต์, Outlaw คาเลอิด, เซลลันผู้ตัดแขนขา.

Should I think about it a bit more?

Deciding to organize my thoughts, I took a break.

Clap, Clap, Clap

As I took off my top and splashed cold water from the river onto my face…

เบลล์ came out of the bushes near the camp… as she started to clap her hands.

It was strange to see the veteran maid, who was always composed and barely changed her expression, suddenly appear while clapping her hands like that.

“You were here, เบลล์?”

Clap, Clap, Clap

“……”

“I brought some ingredients that were discarded during our inspection this morning. I put them on your workbench, so when you have time, you should eat.”

“Well, If we’re talking about taking care of our bodies, then you are the issue, นายน้อย เอ็ด… Aren’t you in a condition where you shouldn’t be working yet?”

I nodded my head roughly as I checked my body, which had no strength left.

I tried moving my body around, but it didn’t move as easily as before. I couldn’t count on เบลล์ forever, so I’d have to get better soon…

“I think I can at least manage the basics in the camp.”

Compared to before, I could now use a bit of magic power. I’d gained enough strength that I can at least carry on with my daily life.

“When the semester starts, I’m sure you will also be busy. So, I need to start taking care of myself again.”

“But aren’t you still recovering your magic power? Won’t you run into problems when you study magic?”

“I’ll just have to try my best. Aside from magic, I also have other things that I needed to pay attention to as well.”

“Other things?”

I looked at the entirety of my camp as I spoke.

“Making the cabin a bit bigger. That’s one of my goals for next semester.”

In terms of magic, becoming capable of advanced magic.

In terms of survival, a major expansion to the cabin.

For now, that was the plan I had.

Aside from that, there were plenty of other things I wanted to do. For example, crafting a special bow, signing a new low-ranking spirit contract, crafting additional legendary magically engineering items, and training my celestial magic… For now, I was sure of my major goals.

If you always had time to practice magic engineering, you needed to set up your own workshop at the camp.

However, if I built a new cabin, the heating efficiency would be lowered, and it would require more effort to manage.

That was why I thought it would be better to do some construction and build a second floor to my cabin.

“I want to increase the number of rooms a little more and make a second floor to use as a study and workshop.”

Even when I was completely exhausted and needed a break, in the end, I chose to come back to the camp.

“I was going to do some cleaning in the villa today…”

“Lady ลอร์เทล is returning to school tomorrow.”

เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์, third year, second semester student at ซิลเวเนีย. Could he rest a bit this break?

ทาเนีย, who was extremely busy with the affairs of the house. เจ้าหญิงเพเนีย, who must have been nervous while preparing for the competition to the throne. And ลูซี่, who went to the imperial court on my behalf. They all must have been thinking about returning to เกาะอาเคน soon.

How would things at คฤหาสน์โรสเทย์เลอร์ be finished up…? For me, who was recuperating on เกาะอาเคน, there was no way to know.

‘No matter what we do, she refuses to return to the school. I hope that Sir เอ็ด โรสเทย์เลอร์ will personally send her a letter urging her to go back.’

“……”

“Who is the letter from, นายน้อย เอ็ด?”

“…Hmm.”

I already knew that ตระกูลโรสเทย์เลอร์ would be dealt with at the Imperial Central Meeting.

Well, it was obvious what had happened.
