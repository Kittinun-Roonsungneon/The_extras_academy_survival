# The Extra’s Academy Survival Guide – Side Story 0: Postscript

- **Source:** [https://bettertranslation9.wordpress.com/2026/03/30/the-extras-academy-survival-guide-side-story-0-postscript/](https://bettertranslation9.wordpress.com/2026/03/30/the-extras-academy-survival-guide-side-story-0-postscript/)
- **Chapter Index:** 251

---

Hello, this is Corita.

I remember the first time I posted this series in the winter of 2020. I was trembling because of just how cold it was. Before I realized it, two years had passed and it was already the summer of 2022. Time sure flies by fast.

During the nineteen months I uploaded The Extra’s Academy Survival Guide, a lot has happened to me—personally and to the story.

Actually, this is the first time I’ve written a novel this long.

Two hundred fifty chapters and an average of 10,000 Korean characters per chapter. But normally, novels can go for around 400 chapters… Now that I’ve finished this story, my respect for established writers has grown immensely.

Just how many hardships have the established writers who have done this over and over gone through?

As this was my first time, I can’t even begin to imagine.

I’ve thought a lot about how I should write my postscript after I finished the novel completely.

However, now that I’ve truly finished it and am writing my postscript, I’m not really sure what to write.

I would first like to greatly thank the readers. The reason I can continue to write something like this is because there are people who enjoy reading it.

I imagined writing a stereotypical thank you with all sorts of flowery language, but the longer I expressed my thanks, the less sincere it felt.

So, I won’t keep on talking about it. After all, it’s an obvious fact, so there’s no need for me to go into much detail.

Thank you so much to everyone who read my novel.

I have never once forgotten that the reason I can keep on writing what I want is because there is someone who will read it.

Since you’ve read The Extra’s Academy Survival Guide, I’m sure you’ve realized it as well, but there are still a few things left over.

The story of the Ultimate Wind Spirit in Pulan. The story of the old fox, Slogg, in Oldek. And the secret between Zellan and Glockt that still remains unsolved.

The protagonist Ed Rothstaylor has reached an excellent ending to his story, but he still lives as an outsider in another world.

I think I will continue telling the story of Ed Rothstaylor and the relationships around him as he completely settles into living in another world.

As for the side stories… They will probably be written as I see fit, just like back when this story was first uploaded for free. In fact, back then I felt as if I was able to concentrate on my writing the most.

There were many times I hit my forehead because there were many things I ended up regretting and realized later, as I ran out of time to write.

Still, I will try and write with the mindset that I should upload at least one chapter every three days, just like I did back then.

However, rather than making a confirmed promise with the readers… For now, I will keep this as a promise to myself.

After taking a week off, I will see you again on Monday, June 13th, with Yennekar Palerover’s Side Story: Wind Flower.

Even though the story was long and there were days I was absent, thank you for reading my story until the very end.

I love you.

(2022. 6. 4.)

From, Corita
