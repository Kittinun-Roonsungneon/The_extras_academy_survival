There were four apostles remaining.

The second seat, Reuben, the third seat, Taldarek, the sixth seat, Clive, and the eighth seat, Lenos.

They felt the difference in their power. Since they couldn’t see a way to win right now, they had to make it difficult for Lucy to deal with them.

So, while Lucy was busy taking down one of the apostles, the rest of them could plan a counterattack.

However, that only worked if they could follow Lucy’s movements.

Then, as the robes wrapped around him burned, Reuben screamed out in pain.

Taldarek turned his head, reacting to the sound… but Lucy had already jumped in front of him.

“What…?!”

The advanced Spatial-type magic, Space Leap, was a magic that consumed a massive amount of magic power. But for Lucy, the amount of magic power necessary for something like that was trivial.

If she were to leap over a long distance, even Lucy would become exhausted. However, a short-distance leap of that level could be used repeatedly, without much effort.

That meant that there was no point in trying to keep a distance from Lucy.

“Keuheuk!”

Then, Lucy grabbed Taldarek’s collar as he looked down at the other two apostles.

She still didn’t show the slightest bit of emotion. Rather than fighting, it looked as if she were taking care of an annoying task.

Clive and Lenos were both veteran Scared Magic magicians that had endured all sorts of hardships.

However, it was the first time they had ever felt such an overwhelming difference in power.

Lucy Mayreel, a genius magician, descendant of the Archmage, and the overall top student at Silvenia Academy.

It wasn’t that they had never heard her name, they had just never even imagined that she would be capable of such strength.

No matter how special she was, she was still a student. It should have been obvious that a student wouldn’t be able to go up against the top magicians of the Holy Father Building.

However, Lucy Mayreel didn’t go to the academy to learn about magic in the first place. Even though she still had room to grow, she was already a nearly perfected talent.

No matter how many magicians of the Holy Father Building there were, it was impossible for them to defeat Lucy Mayreel.

That fact was as natural and acceptable as the providence of this world.

While he had run away for a bit to try and recover, Lucy Mayreel had already defeated more than half of the apostles. He had heard rumors of her strength, but he had no idea that it was at such a level.

Verdio forced himself to stand up as he gathered his thoughts.

The subjugation of the Holy Blue Dragon was an ambitious plan for the sake of the religious group. Every member of the Apostles of Telos were considered to be a major source of power, at a national level. He had mobilized six of them for the mission.

He had brought all of them except for two, who would guard the Holy Capital during the absence of the Holy Father. So, it was no different than them utilizing all the manpower they had.

“……”

However, it was clear what he needed to do.

“Let’s secure a way out! Our priority is to escort the Holy Father Eldane.”

The signal came from Archbishop Verdio.

In case the plan went unexpectedly, he had prepared a plan B—to quickly rectify the situation and flee the scene.

The Apostles, who weren’t able to do anything against Lucy, heard the order. Soon, the apostles that were scattered all around the chapel clenched their teeth as they stood up.

Most of them were crippled, but still alive. Lucy didn’t kill others.

“We’re retreating from the cathedral immediately.”

Verdio quickly judged the situation.

He didn’t know how, but their plan to revive the Holy Blue Dragon using Velbrok’s tooth had been leaked.

Otherwise, Lucy wouldn’t have appeared at such a perfect time to interrupt the ceremony.

If Lucy decided to stand in their way, they wouldn’t be able to summon the Holy Blue Dragon like they had planned. In the first place, all the apostles were already gravely injured. The plan had to be considered a failure.

Since things had become like that, what he needed to think about was cleaning up the mess.

He had planned to resurrect and subjugate the Holy Blue Dragon in order to raise the power of the religious group, and the stage he had chosen to do it on was Acken Island.

Nothing good would come from the truth being exposed publicly. It was necessary to shut the mouths of everyone that knew of it.

However, Lucy Mayreel wasn’t someone that could be silenced through force. And it didn’t seem like she was the type that could be persuaded either.

So, the only choice was to use authority and hierarchy to silence her.

“You’re running away?”

An enormous amount of magic power grew in Lucy’s right hand. It was such a large amount that it seemed unfathomable for such a petite body.

The amount of magic power looked like a mountain. Even the apostles, who had experienced much hardship, had no choice but to swallow to wet their dry mouths.

*Time Prison*. Among the celestial magic spells, it was one that specialized in subjugating opponents.

If you were even grazed by it, your entire body would stop. You would then become unable to move until the caster released the spell.

“Brother Taldarek…!”

Taldarek charged towards Lucy without hesitation, taking the celestial magic power into his body. It was a noble feat for him to take action without the slightest bit of hesitation. He wasn’t afraid to sacrifice himself.

“Brother Clive, take care of the Saint. Sister Lenos, you go that direction with the Holy Father…! The rest of you, follow me!”

Verdio’s head started to spin.

He quickly went up to the podium, grabbing ‘Velbrok’s tooth necklace’. The object would be the most conclusive evidence that the religious group had been trying to resurrect Velbrok. That was why it was also the most important thing for them to hide.

At that point, it would have been impossible to defeat Lucy Mayreel through force.

However, no matter how important of a student she was to the school, she wouldn’t survive against the authority of the Holy Father.

If someone were to ask what the Holy Father and Archbishop were doing there, they could say they were holding an informal service before entering Acken Island and beginning their planned schedule.

If he could manage to hide their plans to resurrect Velbrok like that, then Lucy would become the terrorist that broke into the cathedral, attacking the praying clergymen with her magic.

No matter how convincingly or honestly Lucy spoke, if there was no hard evidence it would be impossible to prove their crimes. And if they were to use the Holy Father’s authority as well, they could even punish her without having to fight. There was no way they wouldn’t reprimand a student that recklessly used magic in a place where the Holy Father was present.

Clive went over to Clarice, who had barely regained consciousness as he grabbed her arm.

“Ugh… Ughh…!”

Verdio held Velbrok’s tooth necklace tightly in his arms as he watched.

“Excuse me, Saint.”

Just thinking about what would happen if the Saint were to come forward and publicly reveal what the Religious Group had done was enough to give him a headache.

That was why it was necessary to get the Saint under control first. They would deal with the school later. After all, the Holy Father Building was the one that gave the Saint permission to attend school. So, the authority to revoke that decision also rested with the Holy Father Building.

Then, when he was heading towards the back door of the school’s cathedral…

Whoooooosh!

Wind began to erupt from her petite body as it engulfed the cathedral. It was just the aftermath shock that was created from the flow of magic power, but the surrounding debris was unable to withstand it as it flew around on the floor.

*Thunderbolt.*

Lucy’s advanced magic could be summarized in that one word. It cut through the air in an instant as it smashed into the cathedral’s ceiling, striking Verdio directly.

It was a relic, and originally a part of the Holy Blue Dragon. It was definitely not an item that would break easily. However, it was small, which made it easy to hide.

Throwing it into the sea… burying it in the ground… it was easy to destroy the evidence. He thought it was a waste, as it was a relic of enormous value, but it was still nothing compared to the shaky prestige of the Holy Father Building.

Taking the corridor that led to the back door of the cathedral and stationing an apostle in case Lucy was chasing after them, Verdio ran out frantically. It was Reuben. He had already been directly hit by one of her magic spells once, so he was barely able to keep moving. He probably won’t last long.

“All… For the sake of Telos…!”

What he needed to do was find a place to throw the tooth away, a place where no matter how hard they searched for it, they could never find it. At least, not within the school’s districts.

The cathedral wasn’t all that big, so it didn’t take long for the back door of the school’s cathedral to come into sight.

Nearly all of the Apostles of Telos had been lost.

The only one left by Verdio’s side was a blonde woman with burnt hair. The fifth seat, Felver.

“Archbishop Verdio… If we keep running away like this, it won’t be long before you’re caught…!”

Clang!

Fwooooosh.

The beginner fire magic, Ignite.

Flames of that level made it hard to just consider it a beginner’s fire magic. Still, it wasn’t unstoppable. But Felver had already been severely wounded from fighting with Lucy.

“Keuk…!”

The ends of the apostle’s robe, which was lined with gold, was set on fire.

*Clang!*

Spiritual Infusion. The explosive magic circle that was pre-engraved in the dagger dealt a fatal blow to Felver, who was already in a weakened state.

At the square in front of the school’s cathedral at midday, all the nearby students were startled as they turned around, looking towards the untimely explosion. A girl fell to the floor, letting out screams as other students ran to call the faculty.

“You…”

Judging from the situation, it seemed he had been waiting for Verdio to come out from there. Anticipating their evacuation route, he had arrived first and waited for him.

If that was the case, then there was a high chance that he was directly related to Lucy, who had raided the cathedral. That also meant he probably knew what was taking place inside.

From there, the path would directly lead to a small forest in the east. Once he could pass through that, he would be able to reach the eastern coast. The eastern edge of Silvenia Academy.

[Young Master Ed…! That guy is escaping…!]

However, Mugg clenched his teeth as he continued to fly, chasing after him.

Ed didn’t want to lose track of him either.

Archbishop Verdio entered the Eastern Forest as he ran, barely avoiding the thickly towering trees that were all over. Although he was quite old, he was rather agile. Perhaps because it was a crisis, he had more physical energy than usual.

However, the majority of the forest on Acken Island was Ed’s territory. Having lived a life of survival out in the wild for over a year, there wasn’t a single corner in a forest on the island that he hadn’t been to.

Running along the trail, rolling down a pathless area, pushing through the fallen leaves, and sprinting across the creek as he ran away.

The thickness of each stroke needed to be extremely thin to form the shape of a letter. It was truly amazing that she was able to control heat so delicately like that.

Not just her insane amount of mana, but also the ability to resonate and control it. He was reminded once again of the fact that she was a girl that had managed to master things to the extreme.

The piece of his shirt that was covered in words flew through the air through the use of magic power. It headed towards the school. Most likely, it was probably headed towards the sacrificial magic circle where Adele should have been praying.

Among the many times it happened, Lucy Mayreel never failed to jump forward, trying to stop the Holy Blue Dragon. She wasn’t confident that she could win, and she even put her life on the line… But there was no way to escape from it.

“I… came to this school to keep my word to that old man… So, maybe I was just waiting for this moment. I really feel like ‘this is the moment I’ve been waiting for.’”

“So, if I’m just going to end up dying after struggling anyway… and it’s completely worthless to try and stop it… I don’t think there’s any reason for me to try so hard this time. Since time will turn back anyway.”

“I want to die in your arms.”

The meaning of those words… Ed didn’t know, either.

Using his body, which struggled to move… he patted Lucy’s back, hugging her.

Lucy put her chin on Ed’s shoulder… she looked up at the sky of a world that was on the verge of destruction.

Then she spoke in a dazed tone, just as she always had.

“This will all be a memory that we’ll forget. One that will never come back…”

“Probably…? That would be the case…”

“Then, I’ll use this chance… to say something I wouldn’t normally say.”

It was embarrassing, but… It also wasn’t overly romantic either. She was only stating a fact as it was.

And perhaps, it was something that Lucy would never be able to confess to Ed her entire life.

“I feel empty without you.”

“……”

“So don’t disappear from me…”

After she said that, Ed kept his mouth shut for a moment…

“…That’s not that hard of a request for me to do.”

As he answered her, he stroked her head.

Then he quietly looked up at the sky.

The Holy Blue Dragons’ scales were like black dots that poured down like arrows.

Ed quietly closed his eyes as he felt Lucy’s warmth.

Then, the scales came pouring down. Among the downpour, Lucy and Ed held each other close and didn’t fight back.

Scales pierced their bodies as blood spurted out, but neither of them screamed in pain.

They only felt each other’s warmth as they bled out for a while… They held each other close.

And like that, Ed and Lucy died.

Even after the rain of scales passed, the two continued to hug each other tightly… As if they could still feel the warmth between each other.

It all seemed in vain, but… It was a beautiful death.

***

“Ed… What’s the matter…? Are you sick…?”

Yennekar’s voice.

“Huh? What?”

“You’ve been in a daze since earlier. It makes sense that you would be nervous, since the S-Saint is staring at you so bluntly…”

“Ah, is that so…?”

I shook my head as I tried to get a grip on myself. I hadn’t been particularly busy recently. Since the Joint Combat Practice was about to take place, there wasn’t any need to go and put a strain on my body.

So, why was I having trouble getting a grip on myself? I was just planning to read my book while resting.

That thought kept coming to mind as I put down the Introduction to Celestial Magic book that I was reading.

Only then did I remember the situation.

I had come here, sitting on a bench in front of Glockt Hall, to wait for the Joint Combat Practice. I came to check the lineup and make sure that all of the duels were happening as I remembered them.

But suddenly, Saint Clarice, who wasn’t supposed to participate in Joint Combat Practice, appeared… and stared at me bluntly…

Then, all of a sudden, she came right up to me.

With an expression on her face, as if she were determined to succeed on a mission, she walked towards me without hesitation. Seeing her act like that, I instantly realized something was wrong as I closed my book and put it down.

“…….”

However, a pounding headache rang through my head, as if it were ripping apart my head.

Along with the sight of Saint Clarice approaching me… there was a strange crackling that sounded like static pushing down on me.

It was like watching a TV with unstable electromagnetic waves… Several images seemed to overlap with each other.

The silhouette of the Saint walking towards me split into dozens. However, I felt as if each one of them were approaching me in a slightly different way.

Dealing with the headache… to gather my thoughts.

I looked down at the cover of the book I had put down, deciding to not read it… I lowered my head.

It was the last book written by the Archmage Glockt, ‘Introduction to Celestial Magic’.
