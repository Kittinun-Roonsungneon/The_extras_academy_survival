“You don’t think I’m an easy woman, right?”

I remained silent.

“I-it’s just… It’s already been a few years since I openly confessed to you. With so much time passing, I couldn’t help but imagine all sorts of things…and…um…well… It’s not a sin to imagine.”

Thinking back on Lortel’s notebook, it seemed that there was a high chance that her imagination was extremely far from reality. In the world of merchants, she was more cold-blooded and realistic than anyone else. But imagining her first time, her imagination was wild and romantic even for a young girl.

“Th-there were many scenarios I had predicted, but…”

“…I forgot them all.”

“Still…This is a rather incredible feeling…”

For the moment, I decided to wait until Lortel’s fingertips stopped shaking. She took a deep breath as she buried her face into my chest, speaking in a whisper.

“The happiness that comes with being hugged by the person you love… It can’t be converted into money.”

“…”

“No matter how expensive the room I’m in, or what kind of fine alcohol or food I enjoy, none of it could ever compare to this feeling… It’s irreplaceable.”

Lortel’s hands slowly stopped trembling. Finally, it seemed that she was starting to relax.

It was just us in this dark room. The only thing we could see was the crescent moon out of the window. The moon shone gently onto the night sea. It strangely seemed as if it was turning away from us that night, as if pretending to not see us.

Lortel smiled under the soft moonlight.

She was the head of the Elte Company who always had an insidious, merchant-like smile on her face. A girl that was always smiling like a fox. I never imagined there would be a day when I would see her look so innocent and pure.

I was sure that she would also be surprised if she saw herself in the mirror.

Then, slowly, my hand came down Lortel’s shoulders as it reached down to her curves.

It was a long night.

Was she afraid?

Every time my hand moved towards an area of her body no one had ever touched before, she would look at me with tearful eyes. She wasn’t hiding her fear.

As if she realized I was purposely being gentle and slow towards her, Lortel wrapped her arms around my neck as she whispered in my ear, “It’s all right, do whatever you want.”

At that moment, I lost patience. I jumped immediately to the point. Time, which had been moving slowly, had suddenly begun to move very quickly.

I could hear her whispering my name. When I came back to my senses, Lortel had flipped me over, now on top with her hands on my chest.

I’d always known that she was a genius and quick to learn. I’d witnessed that many times before, but… Before I realized it, Lortel had taken the initiative and was now on top of me.

Seeing Lortel from below was entirely new for me. She was beautiful in an entirely different way.

Lortel continued to indulge in the unfamiliar euphoria she was experiencing for the first time in her life. She was a woman who always achieved her goals with her own hands. Even in the inn late at night, that forward-looking attitude didn’t change. She wasn’t a person who would sit still and passively accept that feeling of happiness and pleasure.

Whenever the opportunity arose, she would take it. She would try and do everything she could. That was the greed she had towards me, the man that she had her unwavering eyes on for years.

Without realizing it, at some point, I didn’t even need to do anything. As we continued to exchange heavy breaths with each other for a while, we finally reached our limit.

Lortel laid down next to me as she held my face. We finally ended that precious moment of emotions together.

We took a break as we exhaled heavily, trying to catch our breath. While we held each other, Lortel whispered my name once more, saying that the night was still young. I knew what she meant, but for the moment, I needed to catch my breath.

It felt as if the tables had turned.

Lortel then sat on my waist, looking down at me. She looked much more emotional than before. She had a sense of endless excitement in her as if I had helped open her eyes to something new.

Was that how I was going to be devoured?

Even though I hadn’t experienced everything, I would say that I was still decently skilled…

But Lortel’s relentless greed started to overshadow my confidence.

It was true that I had been neglecting Lortel for some time, even when she only had eyes for me, but I had never expected that helping her open her eyes like that would come back at me.

But I couldn’t tell her to stop. I never thought I was the type to worry about things like that, but I guess it’s my sense of pride as a man. Being in front of a beautiful woman who was so enthusiastic and obsessed with devouring me, it wouldn’t be right to retreat.

And I was a man who survived in the wild while Lortel was a merchant. There was no way I would lose to her physically. I was sure of that… But as Lortel hugged me close and whispered in my ear, I had a strange feeling that I was wrong.

“The night is long. There are lots of things I want to try.”

Lortel’s words made me feel as if I were being sucked away, little by little.

The night was a little longer than usual.

I washed my body clean, changed my clothes, and opened the door to go out and get more cold water. However, there was something on the floor of the hallway.

I looked down, curious as to what it was. Looking closer, it was the clothes I had asked the worker to buy.

It was a neat dress that was often worn by female merchants of Oldek. Looking at it briefly, I thought it would suit Lortel well. It seemed that being a worker in an expensive inn gave her good judgment.

But come to think of it, I didn’t hear her knock or anything… The worker must have realized what was happening in the room and left it behind, not saying anything.

I felt rather embarrassed and awkward about it, but it wasn’t like anyone did anything wrong. I quickly grabbed the clothes and went back inside.

On the bed, Lortel had covered herself with the blanket as she wiped her face.

It seemed that she belatedly remembered what we had done last night and was only now feeling embarrassed.

“Ed… D-don’t get the wrong idea. J-just now, I was… I was just wiping something off… I wasn’t embarrassed…”

“There’s no need to be so worked up. It’s natural.”

“A-ahh… M-my back…”

Lortel frowned as she complained about the pain. I handed her the clothes I got from the worker as I briefly explained the situation to her. Lortel thanked me as she quickly changed under the covers.

As for why she was on my bed—it was because Lortel’s bed was no longer usable.

Lortel kept on wiping her face. She seemed to be imagining just how embarrassing it would be when the worker came in later to clean the room.

I sat by the window, I enjoyed the early morning breeze. There was still no sign of the sun rising. Oldek’s nights really were long.

“…Ed.”

Lortel was dressed in her new clothes as she approached the window, sitting opposite me. Lortel and I felt much more relaxed now.

But suddenly, Lortel said something rather surprising.

“…Thank you for being by my side.”

Lortel was probably used to the long, dark nights in Oldek. Nevertheless, she was still afraid of the dark. After all, spending long nights alone only made that feeling of solitude worse.

“Hey, Lortel.”

With that thought, it was time to slowly head out.

“I need to head back to the Elte Company. I’m supposed to meet with Secretary Lienna. I’ll let her know that your physical condition is back to normal.”

“This early in the morning…?”

“Well, there must have been urgent business she had to take care of… I should get going.”

I put on my coat and said, “But before I meet with her, shall we walk down the pier together?”
