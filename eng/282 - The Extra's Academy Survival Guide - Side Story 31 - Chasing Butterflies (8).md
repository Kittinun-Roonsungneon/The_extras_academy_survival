# The Extra’s Academy Survival Guide – Side Story 31: Chasing Butterflies (8)

---

“I…” Lucy Mayreel opened her mouth slowly. She did not understand why the two of them were reacting with such hostility after seeing her, but she felt she had to explain herself first. “I’m… from a different world. I’m not the version of me you know.”

Of course, that alone wasn’t enough to convince them to lower their guard. Clevius continued to glare at Lucy with his teeth clenched. He acted as if he didn’t believe or understand what Lucy had just said.

“What are you trying to say…?”

Rumble

At that moment, Trix Hall, which was now in ruins, began to shake as if there were an earthquake. Towards the middle of the trembling, a massive explosion occurred as dust rose in the air.

Boom!

Fwoosh!

At the same time as the explosion, a burst of heat shot out. A massive beast whose body was covered in flames stood tall. They all watched the intimidating beast as the dust fell down to the floor.

“Shit! It’s a spirit!”

“Hurry and grab your things! If we don’t run away now, more will show up!”

The heat that came off of the beast was so strong that a haze started to form around the area. The large beast looked like a mix of a lion and a tiger… It was the intermediate fire spirit, Veltan.

Its loud roar tore down the few remaining outer walls of Trix Hall, which had been barely standing. However, Veltan’s appearance was strangely different from what Lucy was familiar with. It also used a dark red magic power and had many bizarre patterns all over its body.

Aside from the explosion it caused, it was protecting its entire body with defensive magic.

Doing all of that at once was beyond the amount of magic power a normal mid-ranking spirit should have been able to use. Something was clearly wrong.

However…it seemed that Clevius and Elvira were used to the spirit’s strange appearance.

Booom!

A huge column of fire rose around Veltan. The fire stretched out in all directions, covering the entire area. At that moment, Clevius quickly raised his sword to protect Elvira.

Fwoosh!

Lucy gathered magic power, quickly covering the two of them. Since Lucy knew nothing about what was going on, the coincidental encounter with Clevius and Elvira was her only clue to the world.

Normally, stopping the attack of a mid-ranking spirit was nothing for Lucy. However, she is still unable to fully handle her magic power at the moment. She frowned and clenched her teeth as she extinguished the fire.

Once the flames died down, Veltan could still be standing there unharmed. It stood there intimidatingly, showing nothing but hostility towards them. Numerous spirits could be seen coming from down the hill, making bizarre noises.

Each and every one of them was running around in a berserker state.

Lucy suddenly had a gut feeling.

A person on Acken Island that could handle such a large number of spirits. It had to be…

“We have to run away! There’s a hideout for survivors in a secret library located in the underground waterways!”

In any case, Lucy had defended Clevius and Elvira. They weren’t enemies. As soon as Elvira realized that, she quickly shouted at her.

“If we don’t escape now, more spirits will come. We won’t be able to handle them all.”

Lucy looked towards Elvira and Clevius before she quickly covered the entire area with magic power. It was beginner-rank stealth magic, but it was still effective enough to hide one’s traces of magic power. To escape from the spirits, the three of them ran across the collapsed Trix Hall.

* * *

“Let’s talk about the details after we get back to the hideout.”

Lucy could only nod her head at the statement. From the perspective of the other two, it was unbelievable that Lucy had suddenly appeared. However, they had to reach a safe area before they could answer each other’s questions.

After running for some time, they eventually made it to the commercial district.

Normally, Silvenia Academy’s commercial district would be lively and full of people, but now it was only made up of ruined buildings. Seeing such a sight… It was enough to make a person think that the world had come to an end.

All over the square were the decomposing corpses and skeletons of those who had passed. The marble sidewalks had been completely shattered to pieces and weeds had begun to sprout through them.

In the middle of the square was a large mid-ranking spirit roaming around in a berserker state. Clevius and Elvira were used to hiding from the spirit by sneaking through the gaps between the buildings.

Clevius climbed up the broken outer wall of what was once Laplace Bakery, grabbing Elvira’s hand and lifting her up. Lucy used her magic power to quietly jump up and follow them. They continued to creep along the wall before reaching a destroyed well.

Tons of spirits were passing through the commercial district as they made their way to the North Forest’s entrance. However, off to the side was a large entrance to the underground waterways.

The entrance to the underground waterways was hidden by various bushes. It must have been difficult to cover the entrance to keep it hidden from spirits.

As they quietly made their way through, eventually they reached the underground waterways where Professor Glast had been chased before. After walking further along the underground waterways, they eventually reached Professor Glast’s secret lab. And going a little further…was the secret library that Ed Rothstaylor had used when he was alive.

In her original world, that was where Lucy had gone with Ed to obtain Glast’s phoenix ring. Now, it had become a hideout for survivors who had escaped the spirits wandering around Acken Island.

The students who stood guard at the entrance of the library greeted Clevius when he walked up.

“Clevius. You came back alive. How was your search… Huh?”

“L-Lucy…Mayreel…?”

When they saw Lucy, their faces hardened. Clevius turned his head quietly, telling them to step aside for now. The student standing guard swallowed his dry saliva as he stepped aside. Clevius seemed to be highly recognized and respected in the hideout.

Lucy continued to follow Clevius and Elvira into the library, looking around. The library had already been cleared out to some extent and converted into a refuge for survivors. Tents were set up all around alongside campfires. There were many sick people lying down, covered in blankets.

There were those who were exhausted and starving. Others suffered from chronic diseases. Most were students and faculty members, but there were also some merchants who used to live in the commercial district.

When they saw Lucy following Clevius and Elvira, they began to murmur to each other from a distance.

“L-look over there…”

“Wh-what the hell…?”

“What’s going on? That’s…Lucy Mayreel…”

“She was alive…? But I thought…three years ago…”

“What? Lucy Mayreel is here?”

“Th-that genius magician… If Lucy Mayreel is here…maybe she can destroy the magic circle that is trapping the island…?”

A curious and hopeful atmosphere spread among the murmuring crowd. It felt like people who had barely endured their despair for a while had finally discovered a new light.

Lucy Mayreel took a deep breath and continued to walk forward.

“Inside is the person who is in charge of this hideout. You can hear a detailed explanation of the situation over there. You probably know who she is, but… she’s going to be different than you think.”

The librarian’s room inside the library had been renovated and turned into a room for the head of the hideout. Lucy nodded as she walked past Elvira and Clevius, opening the door. The old wooden door opened with a squeak as she entered.

On the small conference table and blackboard was a large collection of information about the island. Behind all the documents and supplies in the room was a woman sitting at a desk. She looked familiar. She raised her head, her hood down… When she saw Lucy Mayreel’s face, her eyes opened wide.

“You…”

White strands were starting to show in her once reddish-brown hair. It was proof of the hardships she had gone through. Although she was surprised to see Lucy Mayreel, who suddenly showed up again alive, the girl quickly regained her composure as she took a deep breath.

That woman—no, Lortel Kehelland was a person who could remain level-headed and rational in any situation.

“…Let’s hear an explanation.”

Three years after Ed Rothstaylor’s death, Lortel was alone defending Acken Island, which had become a living hell for some reason.

* * *

“Yeah, I can’t believe it, but…”

Lucy explained her situation to Lortel. The fact that she wasn’t someone who was a part of the world, that the Ed Rothstaylor from the world she came from hadn’t died.

However, the fact that she was looking for a way back… She couldn’t tell her everything. It wasn’t an atmosphere where she could.

“About what happened here…”

“You want to ask me why things have become like this…” Lortel had an exhausted face look on her face when she spoke.

With the situation becoming so extreme, she was the one who had led the group of survivors and was in charge of the hideout. Even though Lortel was a capable person, there was no doubt that she was under a lot of mental pressure.

“Fine. I’ll tell you everything I know.”

“…Ed Rothstaylor…” Lucy jumped to the main point.

Lortel lowered her head for a moment before answering, “Ed died three years ago, fighting the Holy Blue Dragon Velbrok…”

“The Holy Blue Dragon… Did it die?”

“…To be exact…” Lortel spoke with a bitter expression on her face, as if recalling a dreaded memory. “He was gravely injured while fighting the Holy Blue Dragon Velbrok. He was later executed by Prince Lindon of the Imperial Household. The reason being, he was labeled the main culprit in charge of Velbrok’s release, which caused the large disaster.”

Lucy couldn’t understand what Lortel was saying. She thought he might have been killed by a direct attack by Velbrok, but…

“…That… What are you talking about…?”

“Those in the imperial household, who used Prince Lindon as their puppet, needed justification to get rid of House Rothstaylor. So, they put the responsibility of Velbrok and the empire’s mainland burning down on Ed’s shoulders…because he was someone who knew that Velbrok was coming before it happened.”

Lucy fell silent.

“Isn’t that funny? Even when the entire world was on fire because of Velbrok, they were having such a pointless power struggle.”

Lortel spoke with a bitter smile on her face. To her, it was something that happened three years prior. The wound might have healed, but the pain wouldn’t have completely disappeared.

“…Emperor Kroel stood still?”

“He died. On the day of Velbrok’s advent…”

“…Princess Penia… Princess Selah…”

“All of the royal family members who were on Acken Island died.”

It was actually strange to have survived it. Velbrok possessed a strength that could blow the entire island away with a single gesture. In fact, it is a miracle in itself that there were enough survivors to even fill the library.

“…What about the top students?”

“Lucy,” Lortel interrupted in a weary voice. Hearing what Lortel said, Lucy couldn’t say a word in response. “…It would be faster to ask who isn’t dead.”

She realized just how painful the hellish road Lortel had been walking through must have been. However…she still had something to ask.

“What about the spirits that are controlling the island…? Did something happen…to Yennekar Palerover…?”

With a sigh, Lortel looked toward the shabby sofa, telling Lucy it would be better to sit. It was going to be a long story.

“…Yennekar… She has become a wanted man by the empire. There is a bounty on her head.”

Lucy went over to the sofa to sit down. Lortel sat back at her desk as she lowered her eyes. One by one, she gave answers about everything Lucy was curious about.

* * *

The Holy Blue Dragon revived much faster than Ed Rothstaylor expected. He expected it to revive quickly, but… It was much faster than that. However, compared to what Lucy knew based on what happened in her world…it had actually revived much later.

From what Lucy knew, the Holy Blue Dragon Velbrok descended upon Acken Island during the Grand Crestle Festival. While the festival’s fireworks lit up the sky, it slowly rose from the sea. She still remembered the calamity the Holy Blue Dragon Velbrok caused clearly.

However, things were different in the world she was currently in. It only received a while after the festival. In fact, it revived towards the end of the semester. There was much more time to deal with it than in her original world. Nevertheless, Ed Rothstaylor failed to stop it in the end.

Why? Was there a difference between how Ed fought it? What was the reason he had failed?

However, listening to Lortel’s explanation… The problem wasn’t with Ed. The more she listened to Lortel’s explanation, the more she felt something was wrong. It felt like something important was missing.

In that world…there was no intervention from the Great Sage Silvenia.

In Lucy’s world, the Great Sage Silvenia intervened in the final battle. It was also Silvenia who weakened the seal of the Holy Blue Dragon Velbrok much earlier, making it revive quickly.

That was why Headmaster Obel, who noticed something strange, did his best to maintain the weakening seal. All of it was because of Silvenia’s intervention.

In the world that Lucy came from, the Holy Blue Dragon Velbrok had come out of the sea while still being wrapped in its chains. Thanks to Headmaster Obel maintaining the seal as much as he could, the Holy Blue Dragon hadn’t been fully revived.

However, in Lortel’s world…Silvenia hadn’t intervened. Rather, the seal had naturally come undone and the ‘fully unleashed’ Velbrok returned.

Velbrok when it was wrapped in chains vs Velbrok fully unleashed… The difference in power between the two was probably massive.

In that world, Velbrok had been revived a little later, but…The fact that it revived earlier than Ed originally expected remained unchanged. As soon as Velbrok descended on Acken Island, it had its full strength, allowing it to split the entire island in two.

In that world…Velbrok was able to unleash its full strength.

However, Ed Rothstaylor still miraculously responded against Velbrok. Despite lacking in power, resources, and preparation… despite the sacrifices of countless students… Ultimately, he succeeded in having the Swordmaster Taylee cut Velbrok.

However, in the process, Daike Elpelan died. Wade Callamore also came to a terrible end. Even Headmaster Obel perished.

Emperor Kroel died defending his people. Princess Selah died while defending her father, Kroel. Princess Penia was killed by monsters as she commanded the soldiers on behalf of the emperor’s orders.

Ziggs Eiffelstein died of excessive bleeding while bringing his lover Elka to the hideout.

Saint Clarice also died after getting caught up in Velbrok’s breath, which swept through the island.

While creating a chance for Taylee to reach Velbrok…Lucy Mayreel disappeared without a trace after getting struck by Velbrok’s breath.

Yennekar Palerover was barely alive after being struck by a storm of scales while protecting the survivors.

And Ed Rothstaylor…fell unconscious after being driven to the brink of death.

However, Taylee couldn’t completely finish off the Holy Blue Dragon. After all, the Holy Blue Dragon had revived too early. Taylee’s specs were barely lacking when it came time to finish off the Holy Blue Dragon.

Even though he couldn’t land a finishing blow…he still managed to land a critical one. Taylee lost his right arm while striking Velbrok, but… He at least caused Velbrok a permanent wound.

Velbrok screamed in pain as it spread its wings wide, fleeing Acken Island. Velbrok had been put in a critical condition. So, before it was chased after and finished off, it had run off to recover its injuries.

However, most of the people on Acken Island had died. Many of those who survived died shortly after. Outside support was desperately needed. When the school asked for help from the Kroel Imperial Household, they responded in an unbelievable way.

Humans are idiotic.

Blinded by their desire for power… Even in the face of a global crisis, they put their own interests first through politics.

Was that the nature of man?

When Acken Island no longer had any strength left… Those in the Imperial Household who gathered around Lindon came and executed the successors of the Rothstaylor family.

Emperor Kroel died, and he only had one successor left. The empire was in a state of chaos.

For those who tried to gain power while using Prince Lindon as their puppet, they first needed to kill off House Rothstaylor. After all, they hold the most influence and power in the social and political climate after the calamity. Those who supported and protected Ed had all been incapacitated because of the disaster the Holy Blue Dragon caused.

It was their only chance, with both Ed and Tanya Rothstaylor in critical condition. Neither of them could fight back. They had to act quickly.

That was how Ed was charged with an outrageous crime and executed right on Acken Island.

That was the beginning of the catastrophe.

Velbrok was almost dead.

The dragon was rampaging across the empire, looking for a place to hide, but it had nearly reached its limit. Thus, the officials in the empire started to believe that they could subjugate it themselves. If they were to organize a team and kill off the dying monster themselves, everything would come to an end. No matter how strong it may be, now that it was on the verge of death, they would somehow be able to kill it.

That would be the perfect chance to obtain all the power of the empire without any competition.

On a stormy day, Ed Rothstaylor, who was at the end of his life, was executed by those who gathered around Prince Lindon with such thoughts.

And, on the same day…

The officials who gathered around Prince Lindon and visited Acken Island all died at the hands of the Elementalist Yennekar Palerover, who knew the truth and had lost all sense of reason.

“Yennekar is no longer the person you knew from before, Lucy.”

The ultimate dark spirit was eating away at her mind and filling it with darkness. The darkness that filled Yennekar Palerover’s head after losing Ed Rothstaylor was incomparable to any other.

It wasn’t just darkness. She even began to lose herself.

Her hair turned white, and her eyes turned bright red. She started to exhibit explosive and rampant magic power.

To make matters worse, after losing, she climbed to the top of Acken Island’s Right Mountain, where the Substitute Altar was located. There, she obtained a piece of Velbrok’s heart, which Taylee had managed to cut.

That piece of his heart was still full of magic power. It was beating with a strong desire to survive. The magic power of Velbrok’s heart, the Berserk magic power that came from Glasskan, and her incredible spiritual resonance…

Between the three, it didn’t take long for the entire island to fall under the control of Yennekar Palerover.

One day, a massive magic circle—far larger than the one during the Glasskan Subjugation—covered the island.

Ed Rothstaylor lived for the sake of saving the world, but the world rewarded him with death.

The empire was so desperate to shove the blade of betrayal into Ed Rothstaylor, the man who had always done his duty and whose life had ended in vain.

Yennekar Palerover could not forgive such a world.

She despised the empire that was blinded by its own greed for power even when the world was in crisis. Caught in the darkness that Glasskan incited, her entire body began to be filled with hatred towards the world.

At least…on the island… The camp that Ed Rothstaylor loved still remained. It was a sanctuary for the man she loved so dearly.

Acken Island had become Ed’s graveyard, a place where he could rest in peace.

And she wouldn’t let anyone get in the way of his peaceful rest.

That included removing anyone she saw.

Due to the guilt over being unable to save Ed and the vengeance she desired against the world that abandoned him, Yennekar became a murderer who slaughtered all the officials of the Imperial Household.

The devil who devoured the entirety of Acken Island.

A broken girl.

She sat on the altar at the top of Right Mountain, holding the large wand that Ed Rothstaylor had used before. The spirits under her, filled with magic power, began to walk around the island.

Meanwhile, she continued to sit at the altar with her head lowered, holding that wand. Red eyes and white hair. All that was left was a hatred for the world.

‘Undertaker’ Yennekar Palerover.

It was a name written on a wanted flier. A survivor had found it by the sea. Yennekar’s reddish eyes on the flier were eerie, and the reward written below was a huge sum.

Lucy closed her eyes after looking down at the flier. For Yennekar, Ed Rothstaylor’s absence was like the end of the world.

“Lucy, let me be blunt. Your goal…is to go back to the world you came from. But…” Lortel spoke in a quiet voice. “At this point… You are like our savior who has suddenly appeared. The only way to do anything about Yennekar, who has lost herself, is to suppress her through force. It’s impossible to talk to her right now.”

Lucy struggled to respond. Lucy in her prime might have been able to do it, but the Lucy right then… The restriction on her power was too strong. Even if she managed to get rid of Yennekar, who was filled with darkness…

“You know…” After listening to Lortel, Lucy slowly opened her mouth. “Hearing you explain the situation… You never mentioned yourself…or how you were doing…”

Lortel Kehelland.

She’s a person who would also have been impacted by Ed’s death. She may have been taking care of the survivors and been placed in charge of the hideout, but…

Lortel… Did Ed Rothstaylor’s death really not bother her? She might have been a coldhearted person who remained cool under any circumstances, but…

At least when Ed was involved, things changed.

She was definitely someone who cared for Ed as much as, or even more than, Yennekar. Lucy was somewhat aware of that fact.

Lucy’s question stabbed Lortel in the lungs. However, Lortel’s voice remained calm.

“…Of course I’m not well…”

The day rumors of Ed’s death spread during the student council election, even Lucy herself was unable to maintain her sense of reason. Lortel, who heard those rumors back then, also looked fine on the outside.

However, after seeing her and Ziggs Eiffelstein, it was clear. Lortel wasn’t one to be consumed by darkness. She would never lose her sense of reason.

Calmly, on the inside, she was cracking under the pressure. You could never tell by her appearance, but that instability that came out once in a while would catch anyone off guard. In everyone’s life, there was a moment where you realized what a broken heart did to them.

In Yennekar’s case, it was obvious that she became dangerous.

However, in Lortel’s case… She had a completely different personality from Yennekar.

“The officials around Prince Lindon may have died, but… Not all his support is gone…”

She couldn’t see Lortel’s expression since her head was down. She still had a gentle and calm voice, but…

“To tear everyone apart, I need to leave the island.”

What she was saying was… a representation of her completely broken inner world.

“Inspector General Albeton. Bellrock of the Imperial Knights, Marquis Vlouren, Saint Pelosis, Crown Prince Lindon, Delos, Elton…”

She continued to say a long list of names. It was incredible that she remembered each and every name. Just how many times had she repeated them?

The fact that she memorized all of them without making a single mistake was enough to give a person goosebumps.

“There are still so many people left that I need to kill as painfully as possible. So, how much longer do I need to stay here, stuck on the island?”

“…You…”

“I am in disbelief that those who agreed to use Ed as a scapegoat are still alive and well, Lucy. I feel nauseous at the thought.”

Lortel raised her head looking eerily calm… She then smiled.

“I have so much to do. I need to leave this island quickly,” Lortel calmly explained.

She was already in a state of disrepair. The absence of Ed Rothstaylor…

It had driven Yennekar Palerover and Lortel Kehelland to obsession over their desire for revenge and their hatred.

It was indeed a cruel world.

* * *

“We’re ready, young man.”

Late at night, in front of Glockt’s cabin, the ‘World Observer’ was ready to operate again. Ed tried to activate the observer again, gathering his Celestial Magic power.

He hadn’t figured out why Lucy had been sucked into another world, but he still had to try everything he could. The growing magic power soon began to fill their surroundings.

“Be careful, young man,” Zellan said to Ed in a serious tone. “As I told you, two different worlds interacting with each other… It shouldn’t be possible. Even though it goes against the basics of magic power, such an unbelievable event took place…”

Ed turned his head as he looked back toward Zellan, who continued to speak.

“…But someone from another world once observed our world as well.”

“…What?” Zellan stood up and looked straight at Ed, uttering, “Someone… observed us…”

Was there someone else who could do that? She couldn’t believe it, but…

Even so, it was impossible to stand still.

* * *

A butterfly flew around.

The butterfly fluttered in the wind before it landed in the corner of the destroyed square in the commercial district.

Reaching for butterflies… It was like chasing a dream that you knew was meaningless.

The butterfly continued to fly around the destroyed square. It fluttered in the wind before reaching the top of a fountain where a girl was sitting.

She wore an oversized, messy uniform. Her witch hat was also worn out. Because the hat was so large, it was hard to see her entire face.

The butterfly sat on top of her finger for a while, repeatedly flapping its wings as it took off, flying higher in the sky. The wings continued to flap as it moved farther and farther away… Slowly, it flew away from her.

Slowly, very slowly, it moved out of her reach.
